## Event summary
- Mobility Q3 2025: revenue +8% y/y despite tariff and regulatory uncertainty; dealer +10% (CARFAX, automotiveMastermind), manufacturing -3% (consulting hurt by tariffs), automotive OEM/financials and other +12%; expenses +6% on advertising/promotion; margin +110 bps to 43.3%.
- The CFO states the company remains on track for key milestones of the spin-off of the Mobility division and will update investors on the separation progress.
- Full-year guidance: Mobility revenue guidance range tightened/raised toward the upper end of the prior range (no figures given on this call).
- On portfolio moves, Cheung says Market Intelligence optimization is substantially complete and future divestitures would be tactical, "nothing at the level of something like Mobility".
- No Mobility-specific questions from analysts beyond the portfolio question mentioning the spinoff.
