## Event summary

- Q2 2025 Mobility: revenue +10%, dealer +11% (CARFAX, automotiveMastermind), manufacturing +3% (low recall transactional revenue, tariff uncertainty), financials and other +12%, adjusted expenses +7%, margin +140 bps to 42.3%.
- Full-year Mobility revenue outlook slightly raised on subscription strength; manufacturing tariff drag more than offset by dealer and financials; margin outlook reiterated.
- Separation: Bill Eager (CARFAX CEO of 20+ years) named President of S&P Global Mobility and CEO-designate of the planned standalone public company; Edouard Tavernier stays as strategic advisor through 30 Sep 2024 (as transcribed). Work on carve-out Day-1 operations underway; timeline 12-18 months from announcement; Mobility Investor Day next year, then roadshow.
- Q&A: strength not mainly tariff pull-forward; CARFAX key driver (100 million customer target for new car listings and Car Care for Life); manufacturing planning hesitancy, tough H2 comp.
