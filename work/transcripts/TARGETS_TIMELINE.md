## Key revisions (in order)
1. **FY26 revenue growth cut** from 7.5–9% (ID) to 6.9–7.7% (Q2). Management blamed the CARFAX bundle go-to-market and weak transactional revenue outside the US. The medium-term 7.5–10% was kept.
2. **Standalone costs moved to the high end**: $20–25M (ID) became ~150bp of margin = the high end (Q2), phased 75bp in 2026 and 150bp in 2027 (GS).
3. **One-time stand-up cash moved to the top**: $75–100M (ID) became ~$100M, half in 2026 and ~50% capitalised (Q2).
4. **Margin-expansion start date set**: '≥50bp medium term after reset' (ID) became 'full 50bp not until 2028; reset complete end-2027' (GS). The base is FY25 40.6% → ~39.1% pro-forma.
5. **Dividend set at the top of the payout range**: 20–25% (ID) became 25% of normalised GAAP NI, $0.06/qtr (Q2/GS).
6. **CARFAX Germany pulled forward** from a planned start of 2027 to early July 2026.
7. **Car Care goal reframed**: 150M US+Canada (ID) became '>100M' US (GS).

## Transcript data-quality flags
- ID 8,656s: '$80 billion-$90 billion per year' should be $80–90 **million** (Q2 confirms ~$89M).
- ID FY26 guide: 7.5–9% in prepared remarks vs. '9% this year' in Q&A (self-correction).
- Q2 H1 growth: 7.4% reported vs ~6.8% 'including the impact of FX' — the wording is ambiguous; given the ~$5M FX tailwind, 6.8% is likely ex-FX.
