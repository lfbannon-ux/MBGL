## Event summary
- Mobility Q1 2026: revenue +8% with high single-digit growth in dealer (+9%) and Financials & Other (+8%), manufacturing +5%; adjusted expenses +5%; operating margin +150 bps y/y to 40%.
- Separation is on track: spin planned for mid-2026, Form 10 to be filed publicly this quarter, Mobility Global Investor Day on May 12 in New York ahead of the equity roadshow, and a public investment-grade debt offering planned this quarter.
- Reporting mechanics: S&P will consolidate Mobility fully in financials and 2026 guidance until separation, then provide recast 2025 and 2026 financials and updated guidance excluding Mobility; current guidance includes Mobility for the full year and excludes stranded costs.
- Outlook: Q2 Mobility growth to accelerate slightly from Q1, stronger in 2H; Q2 margin expansion slightly below the enterprise range; full-year division expectations unchanged.
- Capital: about $2 billion of debt at Mobility in conjunction with the spin funds a cash payment to S&P Global for incremental buybacks and debt reduction; leverage drifts to 2.4x on loss of Mobility EBITDA.
