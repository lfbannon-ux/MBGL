## Event summary
- Mobility revenue grew 10% year over year in Q1 2022 (first full quarter-set post-merger), led by planning solutions and used car offerings; one of three divisions with double-digit growth (with Commodity Insights and Indices).
- Segment margin: adjusted operating profit up 8%, margin down 80 bps y/y, as expenses rose 11% on advertising and headcount rebuild; TTM margin up about 400 bps to 39%.
- Dealer revenue +12%, retention above pre-COVID levels; manufacturing (OEM) +3% constrained by supply chain; Financials and Other +12% on insurance underwriting.
- Management cited higher customer retention as a positive trend; in Q&A Peterson attributed stickiness to industry disruption (supply chain, financing, EVs, autonomous) raising need for embedded data. CARFAX for Life and automotiveMastermind were raised by the analyst.
- 0M of the corporate expense increase was Mobility advertising.

### 6. Peterson, Q&A (Jeff Meuler, Baird: why is Mobility retention above pre-pandemic, and is it sustainable given used-car cycle, CARFAX for Life, aM?)
- Speaker/section: Doug Peterson, President and CEO; Q&A.
- Quote: "This is a time when dealers, OEMs, as well as suppliers to the OEMs, are looking for more and more information about the supply chain, about the supply chain disruption."
- Detail: Peterson says the auto industry is in heavy change: dealers, OEMs and OEM suppliers want supply-chain and dealership-traffic information; financing has shifted (rebates, low-rate loans and leases); EVs and autonomous vehicles add change. Data and analytics from Mobility embedded in customer workflows are in high demand in this disruptive environment, producing stickiness. He did not give numbers or detail on CARFAX for Life or aM specifically.
