### A4. MOAT (revenue mix hedge), ANALYST-challenge
- Speaker: Ewout Steenbergen, EVP and CFO. Q&A: Jeff Silber (BMO) asked about signs of ratings recovery; Ewout pivoted to Mobility margins. Later Jeff Meuler (Baird) challenged that used car is a bigger profit pool than the new-car side and asked about net benefit/detriment and CARFAX innovation given the core report is "fairly well penetrated".
- Quote: "You could say there's a kind of an inherent hedge in that business."
- Detail: Low inventories mean high OEM/dealer margins, driving higher sales and marketing product demand; if supply/demand shifts, used-car (CARFAX) and sales/marketing streams move in opposite directions, so overall financial impact is insulated and margin expansion continues. Mobility "is not an exception" to the aim of improving margins everywhere. Used-car market "has been proven to be quite resilient".
