## Event summary
- Mobility is presented as a $1.4 billion business led by Edouard Tavernier, with a medium-term target of 7-9% organic growth (he is "highly confident" of high single digits) and consistent margin accretion; the range reflects the law of large numbers and long-term investing, with an analyst noting pre-COVID growth was nearer 10%.
- Four growth vectors: planning/forecasting for electrification and the connected car (67 million forecast lines vs a few hundred thousand), digital retail (Polk audiences, automotiveMastermind OEM incentive optimization, third OEM in Q2 2023), used-car (CARFAX listings, Car Care app 28M consumers to 100M+ in 5 years), and S&P cross-leverage (Sustainable1, buy-side/Market Intelligence).
- Business is North America focused, international under 10%; plan to build CARFAX in Europe and a global automotive supply chain data asset; 45,000 dealer relationships with most buying only a fraction of the offering.
- Used car market viewed as resilient; model not tied to transaction counts.
- Cross-division integration: Mobility supply chain data is the 200th Marketplace tile; emissions/transportation data going to financial customers; sustainable mobility team set up with Ratings.
- No spin or separation language; Mobility is treated as a core, integral division.
