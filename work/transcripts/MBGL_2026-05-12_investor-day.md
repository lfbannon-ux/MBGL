## Structured summary (analyst's words; [IR] = management statement at this event)

**Setting.** Inaugural pre-spin investor day (spin from S&P Global targeted mid-2026; completed 1 July 2026). All financials framed on a standalone basis.

**Business profile [IR].**
- FY2025 revenue ~$1.75B, +8.5%; adjusted EBITDA margin "in the 40% range" for three years. Two segments: B2B (Mobility Business Solutions) ~35% of revenue (>$600M, 82% subscription) and CARFAX ~65% (80%+ subscription). Data drives >90% of revenue. ~3,400 employees.
- B2B: works with all of the top-40 OEMs and 94 of the top-100 suppliers; 13B transaction records, 800M+ VINs, coverage of 99% of global production, 85,000 vehicle variants, ~6,000 OEM users, pricing/payment engines in 10,000+ dealers; management says it is under 50% penetrated with existing offerings. TAM $6B, ~10% share, with a path to an $18B opportunity.
- CARFAX: 40,000 dealers, 53M Car Care consumers (goal 150M US+Canada, 1B global), 38B records from 177,000 sources incl. 92,000 service shops and 6,000+ police agencies, 54M site visits/month, 36 OEM CPO programs require CARFAX, ~2M consumer "show me" requests/month, dealers present reports 28M times/month, management-claimed dealer ROI 10-15x. Vehicle History Report (Advantage) is under 40% of CARFAX revenue. Average dealer uses ~1.5 products. CARFAX TAM cited at $26B.

**Strategy [IR].** Three priorities: (1) "One Mobility Global" — combine five previously siloed businesses (separate HR/legal/finance/systems) into one; (2) AI across productivity, data amplification, core-product enhancement and new products; (3) expand market position through core growth, new solutions and international (CARFAX Germany later in 2026; B2B with Chinese OEMs going global, India, plus sales solutions in Europe, Canada and Australia). Cross-sell between automotiveMastermind (3,200 dealers) and CARFAX (40,000 dealers) is a key synergy.

**New/AI products [IR].** CARFAX Lifetime bundle (launched Q4'25; 1,600 dealers; +19pts service retention, +16pts brand loyalty; 14 OEMs give co-op support); VIN-level reliability and "future" content in the VHR; AI Car Fox video reports (25M/month). B2B: automotiveMastermind Behavior Prediction Score (growing double digits; 10% loyalty lift), FAST forecasting tool (automates 90% of workflow, 4x forecast quality), Data Studio clean room / Enterprise EyeQ incentives (+40% close rate, +10% sales). AI Office: 100+ data scientists, 99.5% production accuracy on service decoding, 84% of customer chats/emails deflected to AI, 10-15% engineering lift in 2025 and 4-5x from agentic engineering in Q1'26, MCP/agent monetization in design.

**Financial framework [IR].** FY26 organic growth guide reaffirmed at 7.5-9%. Medium-term (2028+), every-year target: 7.5-10% organic revenue growth (core 5-7% + new solutions + international + One Mobility), 50bp+ annual margin expansion after a reset, 8-11% organic adj. EBITDA growth. Reset drivers: $20-25M run-rate standalone costs (reached in 12-18 months), SBC rising from ~1% to 3-4% to match peers, $75-100M one-time stand-up cash over 12-18 months. Medium-term ETR 25-27%. ~$1B DTL from the IHS merger costs ~$80-90M cash a year for 12 years. Balance sheet: $500M revolver signed; ~$2B bonds before launch, almost all paid to S&P; >=$150M cash at launch; investment-grade, gross leverage target <=2.5x (starting above). Capital return >=75% of FCF; dividend payout 20-25% of net income (keeping Dividend Aristocrat status); buybacks not before 2027; no M&A in the first 6-12 months, tuck-ins only after that.

**Q&A highlights.** Competition: no single competitor across the lifecycle; analysts named Experian AutoCheck (CARFAX) and GlobalData/LMC (Polk/planning). Management called the planning competitor a "low-cost alternative". Data rights are a "mix" (exclusivity not confirmed); value exchange keeps providers (shops lose 22-23% of vehicles a year to ownership change). NRR was pulled from the deck but is "meaningfully north of 100%". Core 5-7% growth is mostly value (price/mix), not volume. Polk serves >90% of US volume. Autonomous vehicles are framed as small (~6M robotaxis by 2035 in a 1.6B fleet) and as change that drives data demand.
