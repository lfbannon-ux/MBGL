## Entries

### 1. Doug Peterson, President and CEO, S&P Global - Prepared
- Quote: "We'll have capabilities to help customers address the big themes that will power the markets of the future, from ESG and energy transition to transportation and engineering, and from private company data and supply chain analytics to credit and risk management data."
- Detail: Describing the pending IHS Markit merger, Peterson said the deal creates scaled, world-class businesses across financial information and services, ratings, indices, and commodities and energy. He said the combined companies would have differentiated solutions in high-growth sectors, with capabilities on big themes: ESG and energy transition, transportation and engineering, private company data and supply chain analytics, and credit and risk data. These data sets would be leveraged across global distribution channels. He called it compelling for customers, employees and shareholders. Transportation is mentioned only as a theme (the IHS Markit segment that includes automotive/Mobility), with no numbers, CARFAX, Polk or dealer detail. Context: he also said closing is expected 2H 2021, with about 99% shareholder approval on March 11, and recurring revenue rising to 76% post-merger from about 70% today.
