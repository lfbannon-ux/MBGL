**Record**
- **SPGI era, revenue:** 2 beats and 1 missed-lowered over three years.
- **SPGI era, margin:** 1 met and 2 missed-lowered, plus the multi-year margin target missed by a wide margin.
- **MBGL era:** one revenue cut within 3 months of reaffirming the guide.
- **Pattern [Inference]:** revenue guides were roughly reliable. Margin guides and targets were repeatedly too high. Misses came from recall/transactional revenue (2024) and go-to-market (2026), not core subscription.

## 2. SPGI-era detail (FY2023–FY2026)

### FY2023
- **Initial guide:** "Mobility 6.5 %-8.5 % … 39 % - 40 %" (SPGI-Q4-22 p.41). The FY23 growth basis is reported revenue vs pro forma 2022.
- **Q1-23 revision:** revenue moved to 8.5–10.5% and margin to 38.5–39.5% (SPGI-Q1-23 p.31–32). Both columns are positional but consistent: Mobility is fourth in both slides.
- **Q2-23:** unchanged (p.31–32).
- **Q3-23:** 9.0–10.0% (pos.; SPGI-Q3-23 p.30). The margin was held at 38.5–39.5% (p.31).
- **Actual:** "+10%" growth and **38.8%** margin, −20 bp y/y (SPGI-Q4-23 p.16, pos.).
- **Verdict:** revenue **beat**; margin **missed-lowered**.

### FY2024
- **Initial guide:** "8.5 %-10.0 %" and "39.0 % - 40.0 %" (SPGI-Q4-23 p.35). Q1-24 left both unchanged (p.26–27).
- **Q2-24 cut:** revenue 8.0–9.0% (p.29). Margin 38.5–39.5%: "given our expectations for softer revenue from the recall business, we now expect margins in the division to be slightly lower in the range of 38.5%-39.5%" [SPGI Q2-24 : Peterson : 2024-07-30 : [1759.98s](https://web.quartr.com/companies/3977?companyId=3977&documentId=2727355&documentType=transcript&eventId=188627&navigation=external&utm_medium=referral&utm_source=mcp&targetTime=1759.98)].
- **Q3-24:** revenue 8.0–8.5% (pos.: the tokens 9.0–9.5 and 8.0–8.5 were assigned to Commodity Insights and Mobility respectively). Margin 38.5–39.0% (p.29–30).
- **Actual:** 8% [D] (SPGI-Q4-24 p.12). SPGI10K: 1,609/1,484 = 8.4% [C]. The carve-out shows 8.6% [C] (1,613/1,485). Margin was **39.0%** [D].
- **Cause [D]:** recall. The 10A shows FY24 "lower recall activity … of $15 million" (10A p.350).
- **Verdict:** **missed-lowered** on both.

### FY2025

### Was the Investor Day reaffirmation already stale? [C]
- **Q1-26 at reaffirmation:** Q1-26 FX benefit "approximately $4 million" [IR]. Q1 OCC ≈ (455 − 4)/420 − 1 ≈ **7.4%** [C]. That is below the 7.5% floor being reaffirmed, using rounded inputs (±0.3 pt).
- The Q1 numbers were public in the Form 10 five days earlier.
- **Q2-26:** 468/439 = **6.6%** reported [C]; ex ~$1m FX ≈ **6.4%** [C].
- **1H-26:** 923/859 = **7.45%** reported [C]; ex ~$5m FX ≈ **6.9%** [C]. The CFO's "approximately 6.8%" is consistent with this.
- **Verdict:** the OCC run-rate was **below the reaffirmed floor in both quarters**. The cut was foreseeable at Investor Day [Inference].

**Reading the implied 2H**
- **Revenue.** The deck says the guide "implies a sequential improvement in underlying growth in 2H" [D] (Q2-deck p.9). The midpoint, +7.1% [C], is above Q2's 6.6%. The low end, +6.3%, is below it. No 2H FX is assumed.
- **Margin.** 2H margin steps down ~80–180 bp y/y [C]. That is consistent with ~150 bp of annualised standalone cost landing in 2H, about 75 bp on the full year [IR].
- **FY margin check:** 745/1,870 = 39.8% and 760/1,885 = 40.3% [C], so "~40%" is accurate.
- **Verdict:** a conservative-looking profit guide, given that FX is not assumed. The revenue midpoint needs re-acceleration while new-business adds were slowing (D7 §5).

## 4. Implications for valuation inputs [Inference]

- **Use ~6.5–7.5% OCC for FY26–27**, not the 7.5–10% medium-term band. The band starts in 2028 per the CFO. History (FY24 lowered, FY26 cut) argues for the lower half.
- **Haircut margin targets.** The predecessor missed its own 41–43% target by 1–3 pt while growing revenue on plan. The MBGL Adjusted EBITDA base (~39.1% pro forma) already includes add-backs (SBC, depreciation) that SPGI's measure did not (D4 §12).
- **Keep cost of separation separate from run-rate.** The ~$100m one-time cost (half capitalised) and the transaction costs excluded from Adj. EBITDA ($78m to date [D]) should be modelled as real cash.

## Honest counters

- **Revenue guidance has been broadly reliable.** Two of three SPGI-era years beat the initial revenue guide. FY24's miss was 0.1–1.6 pt and was caused by a disclosed, cyclical recall downturn. The FY26 cut is ~1 pt on a like-for-like basis.
- **The margin trend is actually upward.** 38.8% → 39.0% → 39.9% [D], with FY25 inside guidance. The 41–43% target was set in Dec-2022 under SPGI's cost structure and synergy assumptions. Current MBGL management did not necessarily set it: the CEO's offer letter is dated 2025-07-28 [D] (10A p.4).
- **The 2H implied guide may be conservative.** It assumes no FX and no macro improvement [IR]. The Adj. EBITDA range embeds the full standalone cost step-up. Q2 margin rose +40 bp y/y [IR] despite stand-up activity.
- **Positional mapping.** Several SPGI slide cells were mapped by position: FY23 Q3 revenue, FY24 Q3 revenue, and FY23 actual growth and margin. A PDF check could shift a verdict by one notch, though not the overall pattern.
- **Q1-26 OCC of ~7.4% rests on rounded inputs.** It could be 7.5%. If it was, "stale at reaffirmation" weakens to "at the floor".
