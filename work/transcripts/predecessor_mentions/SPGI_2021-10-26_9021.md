## Event summary
- This is a pre-merger call (IHS Markit deal pending, expected to close Q1 2022). There is no discussion of CARFAX, Mobility, automotive, Polk, or any separation/spin.
- The only qualifying reference: in the synergy discussion, the CFO says new synergy opportunities have been identified in segments not previously on the list, including IHS Markit's Transportation segment (the segment that contains CARFAX/automotive).
- Merger remedies discussed (divest OPIS, Base Chemicals, PetroChem Wire, CUSIP Global Services, LCD/leveraged loan indices; ~$425M revenue) do not involve the automotive unit.
- Synergy targets raised to $530M-$580M cost and $330M-$360M revenue (from $480M and $350M at announcement); ~200 cross-sell synergies found in a clean room.

## Entries

### 1. Ewout Steenbergen, EVP and CFO - Q&A
- Analyst question (George Tong, Goldman Sachs): which businesses will the increased merger synergies come from, or are they evenly spread?
- Quote: "We now have synergies also being identified in ratings, in transportation, in CMBS, and in CRISIL."
- Detail: Steenbergen says the added synergies are across the board in many categories, segments and functions (a procurement clean room has reviewed about $2.5 billion of spend and 25,000 contracts; a cross-sell clean room found about 200 synergy items). He adds that some segments not on the original synergy list now show opportunities, including ratings, transportation (the IHS Markit Transportation segment, home to CARFAX/automotive), CMBS and CRISIL. No figures specific to Transportation are given.
