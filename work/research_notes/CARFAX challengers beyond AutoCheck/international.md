# International vehicle-history-report players and challengers relevant to CARFAX (outside the US)

Method note: All findings come from WebSearch result snippets and summaries (about 20 queries). WebFetch was not used: many domains are blocked, and the search snippets were enough for the core facts. Where snippets conflicted or came from vendors or aggregators, that is flagged inline. Figures not found are marked "not found".

## Canada: CARPROOF, its acquisition by IHS, Canadian competitors, and why CARPROOF won

### Takeaway
CARPROOF (London, Ontario) was set up around 1999–2000 and became Canada's dominant vehicle-history provider by locking in institutional channels: dealer associations, the Manheim and ADESA auctions, and OEM certified and off-lease programs. IHS (CARFAX's owner since 2013) bought it in December 2015 for C$650m (about US$460m) and rebranded it CARFAX Canada in November 2018. The visible challenger is VinAudit Canada (launched 2020). It is a low-cost reseller of government registration data, not a full history competitor.

### Cited Findings
- **Founding:** CARPROOF was created in 1999 as "LienQuest.com" and was based in London, Ontario — [The Auto Channel, 2007](https://www2020.theautochannel.com/news/2007/05/28/049619.html). Other trade sources give 2000 as the founding year — [Canadian Auto Dealer, 2012](https://canadianautodealer.ca/2012/02/car-proof-named-one-of-50-best-managed-companies/); [canadianautodealer.ca, 2015](https://canadianautodealer.ca/2015/12/carproof-sold-to-ihs-for-650-million/). The 1999 date likely refers to the predecessor name (unconfirmed).
- **Early data partnership:** In 2004 CARPROOF added Experian Automotive (US) vehicle history data, the data behind AutoCheck, to cover US-imported vehicles — [Insurance-Canada.ca, 2004-07-06](https://insurance-canada.ca/2004/07/06/carproof-now-includes-experian-automotive-vehicle-history-data/).
- **Channel lock-in:**
  - In 2007 the Toronto and Ontario new-car dealer associations named CARPROOF their official vehicle history report — [24-7 Press Release](https://www.24-7pressrelease.com/press-release/28937/new-car-dealers-associations-of-toronto-ontario-and-carproof-join-forces-to-give-consumers-peace-of-mind-when-buying-a-used-vehicle).
  - CARPROOF was the sole supplier of history reports to the Manheim Canada and ADESA Canada auctions for US-import vehicles and arbitrations — [The Auto Channel, 2007](https://www2020.theautochannel.com/news/2007/05/28/049619.html).
  - It had a 2012 ADESA partnership for Quebec dealers — [Canadian Auto Dealer, 2012](https://canadianautodealer.ca/2012/03/new-partnership-between-carproof-and-adesa-gives-quebec-dealers-a-leg-up/).
- **Adoption by 2012:** Reports were "a staple of the automotive trade across Canada", used by major manufacturers for off-lease and certified programs and by many dealers. CARPROOF was named one of Canada's 50 Best Managed Companies — [Canadian Auto Dealer, 2012](https://canadianautodealer.ca/2012/02/car-proof-named-one-of-50-best-managed-companies/).
- **Acquisition:**
  - IHS Inc. announced it was acquiring CARPROOF on Dec. 28, 2015 for C$650m (about US$460m) — [canadianautodealer.ca](https://canadianautodealer.ca/2015/12/carproof-sold-to-ihs-for-650-million/); [Benzinga](https://feeds2.benzinga.com/news/15/12/6075810/ihs-acquires-carproof-for-460-million-notes-acquisition-will-be-highly-accretive); [F&I Showroom](https://www.fi-magazine.com/news/ihs-acquires-canadian-provider-of-vehicle-history-reports).
  - The task brief's "~US$459m" was not found as such. Sources say about US$460m.
  - CARPROOF had more than 130 employees and offered history, appraisal and valuation products — [hf.com, reproducing the press release](https://hf.com/ihs-acquires-carproof-corporation-canadas-top-source-of-used-vehicle-data-for-automotive-industry/).
- **IHS rationale:** CEO Jerre Stead said the deal extends the CARFAX model into Canada and called it "highly accretive" to revenue growth and EBITDA margin — [Benzinga](https://feeds2.benzinga.com/news/15/12/6075810/ihs-acquires-carproof-for-460-million-notes-acquisition-will-be-highly-accretive). IHS had bought CARFAX in July 2013 — [Auto Remarketing](https://www.autoremarketing.com/arcanada/carfax-parent-company-buys-carproof).
- **Rebrand:** CARPROOF began rebranding in spring 2018 and officially became CARFAX Canada on Nov. 1, 2018, to use CARFAX brand recognition. Same data, same products — [Newswire.ca](https://newswire.ca/news-releases/carproof-officially-rebrands-as-carfax-canada-699272701.html); [Auto Remarketing](https://www.autoremarketing.com/arcanada/carproof-finalizes-rebranding-carfax-canada/); [Canadian Auto Dealer](https://canadianautodealer.ca/2018/11/carfax-canada-branding-now-official/).
- **CARFAX Canada today:** Sources include motor vehicle agencies, insurers and police. Reports cover registration and branding, service records, odometer, damage, recalls, stolen status and US history, with a lien-check add-on — [carfax.ca data sources](https://www.carfax.ca/vehicle-history-data). It is integrated into Kijiji autos listings — [Kijiji help](https://help.kijiji.ca/helpdesk/autos).
- **Competitor (VinAudit Canada):** A VinAudit.com subsidiary launched in 2020. It uses records from 12 Canadian jurisdictions (not the Northwest Territories), covering registration, recall, status and buyback data — [vinaudit.com](https://www.vinaudit.com/?p=4977).
- **Gap left by US reports:** A BC consumer-protection guide warns that CARFAX USA reports do not include important BC and Canadian data — [VSA BC](https://vsabc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/CFS_Vehicle-history-reports.pdf).

### Inferences
- CARPROOF's win pattern was distribution, not data alone:
  - official status with dealer associations,
  - exclusivity with the two big auctions,
  - OEM CPO and off-lease mandates,
  - lien data (its LienQuest origin) bundled with history,
  - US data via Experian for cross-border imports.
- That gave it the "trade standard" position, which CARFAX copied in the US. It also made building a competitor a better deal than buying one: IHS paid about US$460m.
- AutoCheck's data was inside CARPROOF from 2004. That may help explain why no strong AutoCheck Canada brand ever emerged (inference; not confirmed).

- Pre-acquisition funding or investors in CARPROOF: not found.
- Market share of VinAudit Canada or of any AutoCheck Canada product: not found.

## UK: HPI, Experian AutoCheck, Cazana, Total Car Check, MyCarCheck, carVertical; effect of free DVLA/MOT data

### Takeaway
The UK is a mature, consolidated market. Incumbents own the scarce data (finance, write-off, stolen):
- **HPI:** Solera bought it from Aviva in Dec 2008/Jan 2009 for roughly £78–81m. It is now part of cap hpi.
- **Experian AutoCheck:** supplies the finance data that low-cost resellers such as Total Car Check use.

Free government MOT and DVLA data (MOT records from 2005, including mileage) has turned basic checks into a commodity. That pushes the paid value toward finance and write-off data and toward funnel or freemium models.

Cazana, the best-funded data challenger, raised only a few £m. It was bought by Cazoo for about £25m (Sept 2021), then sold on in Feb 2023 for an undisclosed sum as Cazoo collapsed.

### Cited Findings
- **HPI:**
  - Aviva sold HPI Ltd to Solera Holdings in Dec 2008. Aviva had bought HPI in 2004 — [Aviva press release](https://www.aviva.com/newsroom/news-releases/2008/12/aviva-sells-hpi-to-solera-holdings-inc-4667).
  - Reported price varies:
    - £78.3m — [Insurance Times](https://insurancetimes.co.uk/solera-completes-783m-acquistion-of-hpi/1376298.article)
    - £81m — [AM Online, Jan 2009](https://www.am-online.com/news/2009/1/19/aviva-sells-hpi-to-solera-for-81m/19348/)
    - £112m — [Car Dealer Magazine](https://cardealermagazine.co.uk/publish/exclusive-hpi-sold-by-aviva/3559). This £112m figure may be gross assets rather than price, so treat it with caution.
  - The HPI Check covers outstanding finance, stolen, written-off, mileage discrepancy, plate change, cloning and logbook loans — [Wikipedia: HPI Ltd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HPI_Ltd) (secondary).
  - Solera's UK group includes Audatex, Autodata, carweb and cap hpi — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HPI_Ltd).
- **Experian AutoCheck UK:** B2B focus on retailers, finance firms and insurers. It covers finance, write-off, stolen, exported and scrapped status, with valuations from AutoTrader, CAP, Glass and Percayso — [Experian UK](https://www.experian.co.uk/business/automotive/autocheck/). A 2026 platform refresh reportedly added MOT history, salvage-auction data and mileage history — [Avco Systems blog](https://www.avcosystems.com/blog/experian-autocheck-2026/) (secondary).
- **Total Car Check:** Free tier (stolen, MOT, mileage, tax). Paid checks add write-off, keepers and finance. Data comes from DVLA, UK Police, ABI and SMMT, and "outstanding finance data is provided by Experian Plc" — [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/app/id940049120).
- **MyCarCheck:** Budget option at £4.99 per check, rated less detailed than premium services — [Nerdly review, Aug 2025](https://www.nerdly.co.uk/?p=123121) (single reviewer).
- **Cazana:**
  - Founded in 2012 in London.
  - Funding:
    - About £1.75m in 2017, with Origin Capital and Passion Capital — [Silicon Canals](https://siliconcanals.com/?p=21097)
    - £1.56m Crowdcube round led by Passion Capital in 2019 (about 160% of target) — [EU-Startups, Mar 2019](https://www.eu-startups.com/2019/03/london-based-vehicle-data-startup-cazana-raises-e1-75-million-on-crowdcube-led-by-passion-capital/); [Motor Finance Online](https://www.motorfinanceonline.com/news/cazana-secures-1-5m-in-crowdcube-funding-round/)
  - Cazoo acquired Cazana on Sept 2, 2021 for about £25m net cash, less than a week after Cazoo listed on the NYSE.
    - Car & Classic was excluded from the deal.
    - At the time Cazana had more than 50 staff and over 500m historic transaction records — [AM Online](https://am-online.com/news/acquisitions-and-deals/2021/09/02/cazoo-acquires-cazana-for-25-million); [SEC filing (Cazoo)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1859639/000121390021050852/R18.htm)
  - In Feb 2023 Cazoo sold the Cazana brand, platform and contracts to Percayso Vehicle Intelligence for an undisclosed sum, as part of a "great sell-off" — [Car Dealer Magazine](https://cardealermagazine.co.uk/cazoo-sheds-used-car-data-firm-cazana-for-undisclosed-sum-as-great-sell-off-continues/280224).
  - Cazana was mainly a valuation and pricing data business, not a pure history-check provider — [AM Online](https://am-online.com/news/acquisitions-and-deals/2021/09/02/cazoo-acquires-cazana-for-25-million).
- **Free government data:**
  - The GOV.UK MOT history service shows every MOT since 2005, with recorded mileage — a "free clocking detector" — [fuel-finder.uk guide](https://www.fuel-finder.uk/blog/used-car-history-check-uk-guide/).
  - Neither the GOV.UK MOT service nor DVLA vehicle enquiry shows finance or write-off status — [same](https://www.fuel-finder.uk/blog/used-car-history-check-uk-guide/).
  - Paid vendors use free MOT checks as funnels, e.g. a £12.95 "Hidden History" upgrade or a £10.99 premium check — [VehicleScore](https://vehiclescore.co.uk/motorscan-alternative).
  - HPI itself offers a free MOT history check — [hpicheck.com](https://www.home.hpicheck.com/mot-history-check/).

### Inferences
- In the UK, government data covers MOT, mileage and tax for free. The defensible paid layer is finance data (from lenders) and write-off/insurer data (MIAFTR/ABI). These are controlled by HPI/Solera and Experian, which supply most resellers.
- Challengers that lacked proprietary finance data either resold incumbents' data (Total Car Check uses Experian) or pivoted to valuations (Cazana). Cazana's outcome shows the risk of depending on a single acquirer: an about £25m exit, then a forced resale when Cazoo collapsed.
- For CARFAX this means the UK is a hard entry market: no open history gap exists, and the incumbents control the key data.

### Gaps

- Whether carVertical has a UK operation: a UK subsidiary was reportedly set up in 2023 per an Estonian registry snippet ([inforegister.ee](https://www.inforegister.ee/en/14416655-CARVERTICAL-OU)), unverified. UK performance: not found.
- Percayso's price for Cazana: undisclosed.
- No source quantifies how free MOT history affected paid-check demand.

## Continental Europe: carVertical, autoDNA, CARFAX Europe, Germany

### Takeaway
- **carVertical (Lithuania):** the fastest-growing challenger. It funded itself mainly through a January 2018 ICO (over 15,000 ETH, about US$20m at closing price) and has scaled revenue from €8.5m (2020) to €17.7m (2021), €53.9m (2024) and €75.8m (2025), selling across many EU markets.
- **autoDNA (Poland):** a regional incumbent that wins through marketplace and dealer embedding.
- **CARFAX Europe (Munich, since 2007):** expanded country by country (Sweden, Spain, Netherlands, Poland, Italy), took a minority stake in France's NGC (Jan 2025), and launched in Germany on July 1, 2026 with mobile.de as sales partner.

Germany was historically blocked by data-protection rules and fragmented data.

### Cited Findings
- **carVertical, funding:**
  - In its ICO, carVertical drew over 15,000 ETH; the token's market value at closing was about US$20m. Backers were ordinary retail buyers, not a major investor — [Startup Lithuania, Jan 2018](https://www.startuplithuania.com/?p=2423).
  - Aggregators disagree: one reports about a $19.9m ICO — [Bouncewatch](https://bouncewatch.com/company/carvertical); another reports only $20K over 2 rounds, including Volkswagen's Future Mobility Incubator — [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/carvertical/financials).
  - No confirmed VC round found.
- **carVertical, revenue:**
  - €8.5m (2020) to €17.7m (2021) — [carVertical blog](https://www.carvertical.com/pl/blog/carvertical-podwaja-przychody-w-2021-roku-co-przyniesie-przyszlosc).
  - €53.9m (2024) to €75.8m (2025), +40%, credited to expansion in established and emerging markets — [Forbes Romania](https://www.forbes.ro/?p=502172) (company-sourced).
  - The Estonian entity carVertical OÜ reported a €452k loss in 2022 — [inforegister.ee](https://www.inforegister.ee/en/14416655-CARVERTICAL-OU). Group profitability: not found.
- **autoDNA:**
  - Over 4m reports delivered. About 80% of dealers reportedly pull one before listing.
  - Data from 50,000+ sources in 26 European countries plus the US and Canada, including police, workshops and inspection stations.
  - Free vehicle history on PKO Bank Polski's Automarket.pl — [PKO BP](https://www.pkobp.pl/media/aktualnosci/produktowe/automarketpl-jako-jedyna-platforma-ogloszeniowa-w-europie-udostepni-bezplatna-historie-pojazdu-w-partnerstwie-technologicznym-z-autodna) (company-sourced claims).
  - Poland's government CEPIK database offers free basic history by VIN — [jakiwniosek.pl](https://jakiwniosek.pl/wnioski/komunikacja/historia-pojazdu).
  - Owner and revenue: not found.
- **CARFAX Europe:**
  - A subsidiary of CARFAX Inc. that has produced European and North American histories since 2007. Based in Munich — [Autohaus](https://www.autohaus.de/nachrichten/autohandel/carfax-europe-erstmals-mit-deutschsprachigen-gw-historien-2882945). Managing director and founder: Frank Brueggink — [carfax.eu](https://www.carfax.eu/press/sp-global-enhances-carfax-europe-offering-with-strategic-investment-in-french-vehicle-history-provider-ngc).
  - Local services in Sweden, Spain, Netherlands, Poland, Italy and now Germany — [Deutsche Börse/OTS](https://live.deutsche-boerse.com/nachrichten/OTS-CARFAX---CARFAX-startet-in-Deutschland-Mehr-Transparenz-und-Sicherheit--dcab7029-af2e-4838-8db8-f6e3b99d392f).
  - It has a Swedish entity, CARFAX Sverige AB — [hirebase](https://www.hirebase.org/company/carfax-europe) (unverified).
  - In Jan 2025 S&P Global Mobility and CARFAX Europe completed a minority investment in New General Company (NGC), a French automotive data provider. Terms undisclosed — [carfax.eu](https://www.carfax.eu/press/sp-global-enhances-carfax-europe-offering-with-strategic-investment-in-french-vehicle-history-provider-ngc).
  - Austria: no evidence found.
- **CARFAX in Germany:**
  - Launched July 1, 2026, claiming access to "a nearly complete national vehicle database". The launch coincided with parent Mobility Global beginning NYSE trading on July 1, 2026 as an S&P Global spin-off — [carfax.eu press](https://www.carfax.eu/press/carfax-launches-in-germany-more-transparency-and-safety-when-buying-a-used-car); [Autohaus](https://www.autohaus.de/nachrichten/gw-trends/gebrauchtwagenboerse-carfax-startet-in-deutschland-3824030).
  - mobile.de lets dealers share CARFAX reports (link or PDF) in their dealer area. CARFAX Europe bills directly; mobile.de is a sales partner — [Autohaus](https://www.autohaus.de/nachrichten/gw-trends/fahrzeughistorie-direkt-im-haendlerbereich-mobile-de-bindet-carfax-ein-3904767). Promo: 25% off with code MOBILE25 — [mobile.de](https://www.mobile.de/en/lp/carfaxreport/).
  - CARFAX cites a 45bn+ record database. Reports note that not every damage is reported — [Autohaus](https://www.autohaus.de/nachrichten/gw-trends/fahrzeughistorie-direkt-im-haendlerbereich-mobile-de-bindet-carfax-ein-3904767).
- **Barriers in Germany:**
  - An earlier CARFAX blog post said the service was "not available for German vehicles for data protection reasons" — [carfax.eu blog](https://www.carfax.eu/blog/carfax-story-a-lot-of-money-and-nerves-saved-with-carfax).
  - ECC-Net: registry information generally cannot be obtained without being the owner or having the vendor's consent, for data-protection reasons. Belgium's Car-Pass is the model mileage registry — [ECC-Net](https://www.eccnet.eu/publication/mileage-verification-car-pass-ensuring-accuracy-vehicles-abroad).
  - An older claim that German police believe every third car sold has a manipulated odometer — [FIA Region 1](https://www.fiaregion1.com/?p=192) (secondhand, treat cautiously).

### Inferences
- carVertical shows that a challenger can win in fragmented, registry-poor European markets. It did so through a multi-country aggregation of many smaller sources, aggressive consumer marketing, and many local-language sites, funded by retail ICO money rather than VC. Its about €76m revenue (2025) likely exceeds CARFAX Europe's visible scale (inference; CARFAX Europe revenue not found).
- Winning in continental Europe takes dealer and marketplace embedding: autoDNA with Automarket.pl and the dealer pre-listing habit; CARFAX with mobile.de. Germany was the last big gap because data protection limited access to keeper and registry data. CARFAX's July 2026 launch, tied to mobile.de, is a direct move against carVertical and autoDNA there.
- Free government data (Poland's CEPIK, the UK MOT service) lowers willingness to pay for basic checks.

- autoDNA ownership and revenue: not found.

## Australia/NZ, Japan and other markets

### Takeaway
- **Australia:** The market is built on a government register, the PPSR, which offers finance, stolen and write-off checks for a few dollars. Equifax's CarHistory.com.au (started by Veda in 2009–2010) layers PPSR data with Equifax's National Vehicle Information System (NVIS).
- **Japan:** Auction inspection sheets act as the de facto history record. No national consumer history-report database was identified.

### Cited Findings
- **CarHistory.com.au:**
  - Veda set up Australia's first online automotive bureau in 2009 and rebranded it CarHistory in 2010 — [Equifax AU](https://www.equifax.com.au/node/90).
  - The trademark is owned by Equifax Pty Ltd (filed 23 Jun 2011) — [IP Australia](https://search.ipaustralia.gov.au/trademarks/search/view/1432784/details).
  - Data comes from the PPSR plus the Equifax-run National Vehicle Information System — [Equifax AU](https://www.equifax.com.au/node/321).
  - Older pages call it part of the "Veda group" — [Equifax NZ](https://www.equifax.co.nz/node/90). The newer listing says "part of Equifax" — [Commission Factory](https://www.commissionfactory.com/advertiser-directory/carhistory-affiliate-program/50082). How it passed from Veda to Equifax, and when, is not in the snippets (it was presumably through Equifax buying Veda; unverified).
- **PPSR:**
  - Reported prices are $2 — [Cars24 AU](https://www.cars24.com.au/car-advice/hidden-costs-of-buying-a-used-car-in-australia/) — and $3.40 per certificate — [ppsrcheck.com.au](https://www.ppsrcheck.com.au/node/30). The two sources conflict.
  - PPSR is not a full accident history — [aucn.net.au](https://www.aucn.net.au/ppsr-check).
  - REVS checks preceded the PPSR — [ppsrcheck.com.au](https://www.ppsrcheck.com.au/node/30).
  - Only NSW and Victoria allow government history checks — [Youi](https://www.youi.com.au/car-insurance/articles/car-accident-check-history).
- **Japan:**
  - Auction sheets record grade, mileage, a damage map and inspector comments — [Nobuko Japan](https://nobukojapan.com/japanese-car-blog/how-to-check-a-japanese-cars-history-before-you-buy-a-5-step-guide); [V8 Register](https://v8register.net/subpages/RV8auctionreports.htm).
  - Verification services in export markets, e.g. PakWheels in Pakistan — [PakWheels](https://www.pakwheels.com/auctions).
  - No government-backed Japanese consumer history-report database was found.

### Inferences
- Where governments run a cheap, authoritative central register (Australia's PPSR, Poland's CEPIK, the UK's MOT data), the paid-report business moves to whoever adds proprietary layers (credit bureaus such as Equifax or Experian) or embeds in transactions.
- Credit bureaus (Experian in the UK and US, Equifax in Australia) are natural incumbents. They already hold lender finance data, which is the most valuable field in a history check.

### Gaps
- New Zealand players (e.g., Motorweb, CarJam): not researched or found.
- carVertical's Australia operations: not found.
- Japanese domestic history services (e.g., from AIS or JAAI) and their revenue: not found.
