- Mobility is mentioned only in passing in three places; there is no standalone Mobility discussion, no metrics and no spin or separation language.

- On 2025 guidance (5-7% revenue growth, up to 100 bps margin expansion), she says Commodity Insights and Mobility show continued momentum from core products and new-product investment, with good margin expansion.
