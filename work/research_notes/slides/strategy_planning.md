1. **Vehicle & Supply Chain Forecasting**: light and medium/heavy vehicle sales and production forecasts, down to component and technology level. Includes cycle plans, powertrain and sustainability outlooks, and supply-chain mapping.

## 2. Lay explanation (for a 15-year-old)

Strategy & Planning is the car industry's **weather forecast**. It predicts how many of each car model will be built and sold, in every country, years ahead, and which parts and suppliers will go into them. Carmakers and parts makers use it to decide which factories to build, how many parts to order, and which technology to bet on.

- **Example 1:** Toyota wants to know how many Camrys (and hybrid vs petrol versions) will sell in the US in 2028, and which plant should build them. The forecast gives model-by-model numbers.
- **Example 2:** A seat-maker such as a big supplier wants to know which of its customers' models will grow, so it can plan factory capacity and bid for contracts. Over 2,900 supplier plants and 4,400 suppliers are mapped.
- **Example 3 (FAST / PIQ):** "What if a 25% tariff hits Mexican imports?" or "what if we raise the price by $5,000?" FAST reruns the forecast in minutes. PIQ shows which alternative suppliers could make a part elsewhere.

## 3. How it makes money

- **Subscription:** "Planning Solutions (vehicle & supply chain forecasting, powertrain and technology, global reporting)" sold on monthly, annual or multi-year subscriptions [D, 10-Q p.20].
- **Transactional:** "Planning Solutions one-time data deliveries", plus "consulting and advisory services" [D, 10-Q p.20]. Projects are discretionary and can slip (Q2-26: "modest delays in Planning Solutions projects").
- **Sales model:** "a dedicated global sales organization that sells directly to OEMs, suppliers and financial markets", backed by "a robust, in-market team of analysts". The foundation (forecasts) lets the team upsell FAST, PIQ and DA [D, 10/A p.381].
- **Pricing:** no price points or escalators disclosed. The FAST value claim is "$100k+ customer productivity gain per planner per year" and "80% time savings" (internal data plus McKinsey/Deloitte/IBM research) [D-deck s.49].
- **Subscription resilience claim:** "When the dust settled in those most challenging years, our subscription revenue for OEMs and suppliers actually grew." ## 6. Moat in plain words

1. **Scale and detail that are slow to copy:** a 195-analyst team, 85,000 variants and supplier mapping to the plant. The rival is described as cheaper and less detailed [D; IR above].
2. **Two-way feedback:** "reciprocal relationships with OEMs, suppliers and financial institutions" enrich the data [D, 10/A p.378]. The deck shows "Bidirectional Communication" and "Industry Point of Reference" (s.42).
3. **Common currency:** the forecasts are embedded in OEM "production planning, allocation and supply chain scenario modeling" (s.39) and used in supplier tender bidding (s.43). Switching means re-basing every internal plan. *(This is the filing's claim; no churn data is given.)*
4. **Volatility helps:** "We thrive in stable periods, a lot of organizations do, but we also excel in times of uncertainty and volatility because this is the time when some of those decisions are going to matter the most." LaFeir, ID26 ([3302.56s](https://web.quartr.com/companies/22709?companyId=22709&documentId=3659313&documentType=transcript&eventId=676565&navigation=external&utm_medium=referral&utm_source=mcp&targetTime=3302.56)). S&P said the same in 2022: "on the manufacturing side, high levels of uncertainty relating to the supply chain are driving demand for our forecast offerings." Ewout Steenbergen, CFO, S&P Global, 1 Mar 2022 ([1634.72s](https://web.quartr.com/companies/3977?companyId=3977&documentId=3735411&documentType=transcript&eventId=16001&navigation=external&utm_medium=referral&utm_source=mcp&targetTime=1634.72))

**Honest limit:** independent evidence is very thin. One OEM user confirms the subscription: "S&P Global, the parent company, we subscribe to their automotive and global automotive insights…" [X #4, Nissan NMAC finance manager / ex-CARFAX rep, 23 Jun 2026, p.6]. ## 10. Risks (with quotes)

- **AI commoditising forecasts:** "Our competitors are deploying AI in ways that could materially reduce demand for our products and services (for example, by deploying AI in ways that make collection and processing of information relatively inexpensive or free…)" [D, 10/A p.297]. Expert view on customer budgets (generic, not planning-specific): "all of their bosses are saying, 'Just use AI to do it all. We don't need to spend any more money.'" [X #2, ex-Cox AVP Product, 9 Jul 2026, p.4]
- **Slow history (old SPGI "Manufacturing" line):** the OEM/supplier line grew in low single digits outside recall swings, e.g. "+3%" in Q1-22 and Q2-22 (constrained by supply chain; "flat OEM marketing spend") and −3% to +3% in 2024–Q2-25 [IR, SPGI calls].
