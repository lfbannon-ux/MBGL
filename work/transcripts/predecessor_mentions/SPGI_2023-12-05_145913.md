## Event summary
- The moderator framed Mobility as the strongest growth segment, guided up 9-10% for full-year 2023, driven by CARFAX, CARFAX for Life and automotiveMastermind.
- Steenbergen sees no reason for the high growth to change: a once-in-a-century industry shift to EVs and hybrids drives demand for Mobility planning/forecasting solutions.
- Normalizing retention (inventory rising) is offset by OEMs and dealers spending more on marketing, which benefits automotiveMastermind, the marketing engine behind many dealers and OEMs.
- Business described as well run, highly entrepreneurial with a steady cadence of new product launches; outlook positive for the next phase.
- No spin or separation commentary; portfolio-simplification remark at the end was general (not Mobility specific).

## Entries

### 1
- Speaker / section: Ewout Steenbergen, EVP and CFO; Q&A. Moderator (Goldman Sachs) asks how sustainable current growth is within Mobility, the strongest segment, guided up 9-10% for the year and driven by CARFAX, CARFAX for Life and automotiveMastermind.
- Quote:
> "It's one of the high growth businesses in the portfolio, and there's no reason, from my perspective, to believe that that will change-"
- Detail: First reason is structural: the biggest revolution in the industry in about a century as traditional carmakers move to EVs and hybrids and must rethink production lineups, supply chains, factory locations and battery sourcing. They need data and research support, which Mobility's planning solutions provide, and industry upheaval is generally good for S&P. Framing figures (from the question): 9-10% full-year growth guide.
