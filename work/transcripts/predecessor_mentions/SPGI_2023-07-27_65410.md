## Event summary
- Mobility revenue +10% y/y in Q2 2023 (high single digits per Peterson's headline), driven by CARFAX new business, MarketScan contribution in dealer, and strong financials/underwriting volumes.
- Dealer +12% (price increases, new stores, CARFAX for Life and used car subscriptions); Manufacturing +5% (elevated recall activity, marketing solutions); Financials and Other +9%.
- Margin: expenses +13% (including MarketScan inorganic), adjusted operating profit +5%, margin down 160 bps y/y, TTM margin contraction of 60 bps; MarketScan adds about 150 bps of Mobility revenue growth in 2023 but is modestly margin-dilutive; full-year Mobility guidance unchanged.
- Tavernier said retention and sales cycles softened slightly in H1 after elevated prior years, now stabilized; retention remains very strong. He also described white space for young products (CARFAX for Life, Used Car Listings, aM, Supply Chain and Technology), 30-50% penetrated.
- AI/ESG: Mobility launched an internal GenAI tool "Autopilot" in July; Climanomics automotive physical-risk version launched in June; a carbon-accounting dataset for automotive (scope 3) is in build.
