## Entries

### 1. Mobility data made available to ratings analysts
- Speaker/section: Martina Cheung, President, S&P Global Ratings; Q&A (Balsky asked how the IHS Markit merger, two years on, has benefited Ratings and what opportunities lie ahead)
- Quote: "So think about the mobility data, the commodity insights data, some of the fixed income data, that Market Intelligence, for example."
- Detail: Cheung says two years in, the merger has helped Ratings in three ways: (1) shared-services cost savings, such as renegotiated third-party licenses; (2) data, with high-value IHS Markit data, including Mobility data, now accessible to ratings analysts, who previously compiled data manually or bought it from third parties. This also gives extra reference points and supports additional research, which draws investor attention and ultimately demand for ratings; (3) complementary IHS Markit products enabling more referrals from the Ratings commercial team to other divisions, tracked but smaller in scale than cross-sell in the merging divisions. Mobility is named only as a data source; no sizing.
