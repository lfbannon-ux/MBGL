## Event summary
- Mobility revenue grew 8% YoY in Q2 2024 (9% excluding the divested after-sales business): Dealer +11% (new car listings, CARFAX), Manufacturing -1% (soft one-time recall revenue), Financials & Other +13% (record underwriting volumes). Operating profit +10%, margin +60 bps to 40.9%; TTM margin down 10 bps to 38.8%.
- Subscription revenue is about 81% of Mobility revenue and grew 10.5%; recall is described as high margin but non-recurring and abnormally soft, which is the main drag.
- Guidance: Mobility revenue growth range tightened and slightly lowered to 8%-9% (from 8.5%-10%), and margin guidance trimmed 50 bps to 38.5%-39.5%, both attributed to expected continued softness in recall.
- CARFAX listings and the CARFAX Banking & Insurance Group were cited as key contributors to Vitality revenue ($375M, 11% of total revenue).

### 4. Prepared - Doug Peterson, President and CEO, S&P Global - [Mobility revenue guidance]
- Quote: "We're slightly lowering and tightening the guidance range for Mobility revenue growth. As we noted last quarter and this morning, the recall business has been abnormally soft year-to-date, and we expect that softness to continue in the second half."
- Detail: Peterson explains that recall revenue is non-recurring and hard to predict. With the lowered outlook, the rest of the Mobility revenue is more predictable recurring subscription, giving confidence in a tighter revenue growth range of 8%-9%, versus the prior 8.5%-10%. Company-wide, the full-year guide was raised (revenue growth 8%-10%, adjusted operating margin expansion of 125-175 bps, adjusted EPS $14.35-$14.60). Market Intelligence revenue 6%-7.5% unchanged; Indices raised to 10%-12%.

### 6. Q&A - Chris Craig, Interim CFO, S&P Global - Jeff Meuler (Baird) asks whether there is anything in Mobility beyond recall weakness (e.g., subscription bookings trends, CDK ransomware impact on clients), given the margin guide was lowered and recall is thought to be lower-margin.
- Quote: "When you look back, 81% of the revenue is driven out of subscription growth, and that grew up 10.5%. In terms of the recall activity, it's actually a very high margin business that comes through."
- Detail: Craig says subscription growth was "tremendous" this quarter: subscription is 81% of Mobility revenue and grew 10.5%. He corrects the analyst's premise: recall is a very high-margin business, so the loss and slowdown in recall hits margins fairly significantly. He does not address the CDK ransomware point and gives no sign of change in subscription bookings trends.
