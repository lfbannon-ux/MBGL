## 2. Lay explanation (for a 15-year-old)

Every car has a 17-character ID number (the VIN). CARFAX collects facts about each VIN from police, repair shops, dealers and state DMVs: crashes, oil changes, how many owners, the mileage at each visit. It prints them as a "vehicle history report". **Advantage** is the monthly subscription a car dealer pays so it can pull and hand out as many of these reports as it needs, free to the shopper.

- A dealer selling a used 2019 Honda Civic attaches the CARFAX report to the online listing to prove the car has one owner, no accidents and regular oil changes, so the buyer trusts it and pays the asking price.
- A dealer looking at a trade-in pulls the report and sees the odometer reading from a 2023 oil change was higher than today's dashboard number: a rolled-back odometer, so it does not buy the car.
- A shopper walks in and says "Show me the CARFAX" (management: about 2 million times a month [D-deck slide 67]). If the dealer hands over a different brand of report, the shopper may get suspicious.

## 3. How it makes money

- **Unit:** monthly subscription per dealer location (rooftop); price tiers by report volume up to unlimited. Revenue drivers per filing: "the number of dealer locations enrolled in dealer subscription products (Advantage, Car Listings, and CARFAX For Life), the average monthly price per location on each product" [D, Form 10/A p.340].
- **Subscription, not transactional:** CARFAX is 81% subscription [D].
- **Growth is mostly price:** 6M-26 CARFAX revenue +$46m "primarily driven by price increases" [D, 10-Q Q2-26 p.28]. Group-level: price ~$36m of $64m 6M-26 growth (~56%) [D 10-Q p.27 / C].
- **Price points (experts, unaudited):**
  - Single rooftop unlimited: "If you want unlimited and you are just a one-rooftop store, it might just be like $999." [X #4, p.8]; capped tiers "$250 a month, $500 a month, $750, $1,000" [X #4, p.8].
  - AutoNation luxury store, bundle of Advantage + Listings + For Life: "We pay under $5,000 a month." [X #6, p.3]
  - Annual list increases "up to 5%-8%"; small groups ~8%, large groups (Sonic, AutoNation, Lithia) "in your 5%s" [X #1, p.3]; AutoNation store flat for 2+ years [X #6, p.3].
  - Usage true-ups for heavy/automated pullers: "$2,500 a month to $4,800 a month to like $6,800 a month" [X #2, p.7].
- **Management value claim:** dealers see "~9% faster inventory turn", "~5% more gross profit", "10-15x ROI" (CARFAX 2023 economic model study) [D-deck slide 68]; "This translates into 10 to 15x ROI." [IR, Fredericks, Investor Day, [5993.17s](https://web.quartr.com/companies/22709?companyId=22709&documentId=3659313&documentType=transcript&eventId=676565&navigation=external&utm_medium=referral&utm_source=mcp&targetTime=5993.17)]

## 6. Moat (plain words)

1. **Shoppers ask for it by name.** 96% awareness; ~2M "Show me the CARFAX" requests a month [D-deck]. "The consumer walked in and said, "Show me the CARFAX." You want to put in brand Z in front of them, the dealer loses confidence with the consumer." [IR, Fredericks, Investor Day, [9459.13s](https://web.quartr.com/companies/22709?companyId=22709&documentId=3659313&documentType=transcript&eventId=676565&navigation=external&utm_medium=referral&utm_source=mcp&targetTime=9459.13)]
2. **Data rivals do not have.** Service and odometer records from 92,000+ shops (given a free tool in exchange for data) and police crash reports from 6,300+ agencies [D 10/A p.263]. "The advantage that CARFAX has is CARFAX has all the odometer readings." [X #1, p.9]
3. **Built into dealer workflow.** 95 dealer-tool integrations, 36 OEM CPO programs [D-deck].
4. **Not worth copying.** Experian costed a rebuild at 3-5 years for 20-25% share and "didn't see the ROI" [X #1, p.6].
- Limits: only ~20% of collision data is unique ("My best guess would be 20% would be unique, 80% would be common." [X #5, p.4]); exclusivity is contractual and "a mix" [IR, Eager, Investor Day 9695.18s]; courts treated the lead as consumer preference, not contractual lock-in [court].

## 7. Fit with the rest of Mobility Global
- **Hub product:** "You have to have Advantage." Dealers add Car Listings and CARFAX For Life (Service Loyalty) on top; average 1.5-2 products per dealer [IR Q2-26].
- **Data loop:** dealers and shops send service data in (give-get); the same data powers Car Care (53M+ users), Listings ("Every vehicle listing includes a free CARFAX report, history-based value and verified reviews." [D 10/A p.263]) and BIG (lenders/insurers, sold per VIN).
- **Cross-sell with automotiveMastermind:** aM's 3,200 dealers vs CARFAX's 40,000; plan to "take the offers for those 3,200 dealers and activate them in our Car Care platform" [IR, Eager, Q2-26, [2800.63s](https://web.quartr.com/companies/22709?companyId=22709&documentId=4111696&documentType=transcript&eventId=704668&navigation=external&utm_medium=referral&utm_source=mcp&targetTime=2800.63)]. Experts report separate sales forces (#5, #6).
- **Platform for International:** CARFAX Germany launched on the "CARFAX U.S. ingestion and insights platform" [IR, Eager, Q2-26, 2273.8s].
- **Bundle lesson:** Lifetime bundle (Advantage + Listings + Service Loyalty) lengthened sales cycles ("two yeses") and was unwound in June 2026 [IR].

## 10. Risks / frustrations

- **Large groups flat:** "I want to say it's been the same for at least the past two years that I can remember. We haven't had a price increase." [X #6, p.3]
- **AutoCheck distribution gains:** marketplaces (KBB/Autotrader Jun-2025; AIM Group Jan-2026); management: "I think that our space has always been a competitive market. From time we see different pockets of increased competition." [IR, Eager, Q2-26, [3615.03s](https://web.quartr.com/companies/22709?companyId=22709&documentId=4111696&documentType=transcript&eventId=704668&navigation=external&utm_medium=referral&utm_source=mcp&targetTime=3615.03)]
- **"Good enough" risk:** "Plenty of dealers, to your point, you hand them a vehicle history report that's AutoCheck, Experian-branded, and I don't think the consumer is going to say, "What is this? Where is the CARFAX?"" [X #2, p.4]
