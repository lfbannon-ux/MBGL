### 2. Mark Grant, SVP Investor Relations and Treasurer - Q&A (answer, two turns)
- Question: Same capital strategy question as above.
- Quote: "With the completion of the mobility spin, which we expect to take place on July 1st, you're right, we've raised $2 billion of debt financing that gets dividended out to S&P Global. We want to make sure that the spin's leverage neutral for us."
- Detail: The $2B of debt financing raised by Mobility Global is dividended to S&P Global; stated use of proceeds is share repurchases and some debt reduction, and the spin is meant to be leverage neutral. The public capital-return target is at least 85% of free cash flow annually via dividends and buybacks; with the Mobility proceeds it is raised to 100% in 2026. That works out to roughly $4.5 billion of repurchases this year, the main focus given valuation and the high return on buying back stock. That raises the bar for tuck-in M&A; they have said there is no appetite for anything transformational and the appetite for tuck-ins has diminished somewhat.
