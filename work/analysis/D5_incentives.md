## 3. MBGL plans after the spin

### 3.1 2026 Long Term Incentive Plan (10/A pp.407-410 [146-149]; adopted per 10-Q p.18; full text in the S-8 Ex.99.1, not in Quartr)

- **Share reserve** [D]: 14% of shares outstanding immediately after the Distribution. The ISO limit is also 14%.
  - On 294,821,320 shares (10-Q p.11) this is about **41.3m shares** [C].
  - At an illustrative 3-4% annual burn this would last 3.5-4.7 years. That burn rate is our assumption, not disclosed [E].
  - The 10/A notes that the reserve "will initially be subject to increase as described below", but no evergreen clause appears in the Quartr text [D/gap].
- **Share-counting and replacement awards** [D]:
  - The plan uses **liberal share recycling**. Shares withheld for tax or exercise price go back into the pool.
  - **Converted Mobility RSUs and acquisition replacement awards do not count** against the reserve.
- **Award types** [D]: RSUs, PSUs, options, SARs, restricted stock, deferred awards, and other stock- and cash-based awards.
- **No performance metrics are specified in the plan.** The N&C Committee has sole discretion, and it **may amend outstanding awards** (p.410) [D].
- **Change in control (CIC)** [D]: treatment is discretionary. It may be continuation or assumption, substitution, **acceleration**, a committee determination of performance attainment, or a cash-out.
  - CIC is defined as 50% ownership, a board turnover, a merger or sale, or liquidation.
  - A **single trigger is not ruled out** in the plan text [E].
- **Clawback** [D]: there is a recoupment policy as required by listing rules. Hedging and pledging are prohibited (10/A pp.394-395).

### 3.2 Conversion of SPGI awards (10/A pp.393-394; 10-Q p.18) [D]

At 15-May-2026, Mobility participants held:
- SPGI RSUs on **102,529** shares;
- SPGI PSUs on **43,812** shares at target.

Conversion rules:
- **RSUs** become MBGL RSUs on the same vesting terms, with intrinsic value preserved.
- **PSUs convert into time-based MBGL RSUs.** In-flight cycles convert "based on actual performance through the Distribution Date". **PSUs granted in the separation year (2026) convert at target.** No further performance test applies [D].
  - In effect, the 2024-26 and 2025-27 CARFAX and SPGI PSUs, and the **entire 2026 LTI**, became time-vested equity with no performance hurdle [E, inferred from p.394].

The SPGI value converted is about $76m at the FYE25 price of $522.59, with PSUs at target [C]. The MBGL share count depends on the SPGI/MBGL price ratio at the spin, which is not in hand [gap].

### 3.3 Annual Incentive Plan, 2026+ (10-Q Ex.10.17, pp.173-178; effective 1-Jan-2026, adopted 24-Jun-2026) [D]

- **Formula** [D]: base salary at 31-Dec × target % × **Company Performance Factor** × **Individual Performance Factor**.
- **Metrics are not disclosed.** "Company Performance Factors may be financial or strategic", at company, segment or BU level, absolute or relative. The committee may "increase or decrease any Participant's Actual Incentive" and may adjust goals mid-year for "other reasons as the Committee may determine" (pp.174-176) [D].
- **Flag** [E]: no revenue, Adj. EBITDA, FCF or TSR weighting has been announced for FY26.

- Payout terms [D]: payment by 15-Mar. Termination without cause or retirement after 31-Mar pays pro rata on actual results. Death or disability pays target.

## 5. Assessment

### What the structure rewards [E]

1. **Unit EBITA and margins over growth.**
   - The historical design paid well above target even when revenue growth missed: Eager earned 120% with both revenue metrics below target.
   - The two CARFAX-heavy leaders, Eager and Fredericks, were paid mainly on **CARFAX U.S. EBITA**. That covered 72% of Eager's 2025 annual LTI and 100% of Fredericks' STI.
   - B2B was barely represented in the incentives of anyone but LaFeir, whose total pay is the smallest at $1.3m.
   - This fits a CARFAX-centric culture of harvesting the cash cow. It also means the CEO's track record of incentive alignment was built on a single unit.
2. **Historic payouts near maximum.**
   - The 2023-25 PSUs paid about 182-184% [C].
   - The 2025 CARFAX PSU band is tight, 4.5% from threshold to target, with a 50% floor.
   - Target-setting at the unit level looks beatable [E].
3. **Retention-heavy transition pay.**
   - Retention cash went to LaFeir ($700k) and Matharu ($216k).
   - The directors' $400k cliff RSUs and the CFO's $5.9m in one-time awards are retention and buy-out in nature.
   - The PSU conversion removed the performance tests on in-flight and 2026 LTI.
   - For roughly FY26-28, **most of management's at-risk equity vests on time alone** [E]. A target $2.95m CEO 2026 LTI became, effectively, RSUs [E].

## Honest counters

- **Absence is not evidence.** A newly spun company with no proxy normally leaves metrics, guidelines and an ESPP undisclosed. All of these may be adopted at the first N&C meeting cycle. Several risks above are about what is missing ([E]), not about bad design.
- **Unit-level EBITA metrics are not obviously wrong for a 40%+ margin data business.** CARFAX is about two-thirds of revenue (Q2: $312m of $468m) [D]. Rewarding its profit pools may be exactly right. Margin metrics also guard against buying growth.
- **High PSU payouts reflect real outperformance.** SPGI's Adj. EPS CAGR was 17.6%, and CARFAX EBITA beat a target set by SPGI's committee, not by Mobility management.
- **Converting PSUs to time RSUs is standard spin practice.** In-flight SPGI metrics cannot be measured meaningfully at MBGL. Converting on *actual* performance to the spin is the shareholder-friendly variant.
- **The SBC ramp is disclosed openly by the CFO.** It moves pay toward equity, which is the alignment shareholders usually ask for. Adj. EBITDA excluding SBC is industry-standard reporting, and the incentive definition may yet differ.
- **Severance terms are mid-market.** CEO 2× and ELT 1.5× with a double trigger and no gross-up are typical. The CEO's CIC multiple is below the common 2.5-3× for large caps [E].
- **The 14% reserve may be sized to absorb the conversion of SPGI awards across the full employee base**, plus a lift in breadth. A large pool is not the same as a large burn.
- **Data quality.** The beneficial-ownership figures, part of the severance table and the outstanding-awards column mapping are flattened in Quartr text, and some figures above depend on our reading of those tables.
