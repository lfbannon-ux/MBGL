# US platform/incumbent challengers to CARFAX, CARFAX's competitive defences, and the 2013–2018 dealer antitrust suit

Method note: WebFetch and curl were blocked for nearly all relevant domains (FindLaw, Justia, CourtListener, FI Magazine, Motor1 all failed with DNS errors or proxy 403s). Everything below comes from **search-engine snippets and summaries**, not full-text reads. Where a snippet was truncated or ambiguous, I say so. I found no reliable figures for investment amounts (how much Cox, Experian or others spent competing with CARFAX). Those are listed as gaps.

## 1. Cox Automotive (Autotrader, KBB, Manheim, vAuto, Dealertrack) and Experian AutoCheck

### Takeaway
Cox went through three phases. It was a CARFAX-exclusive partner from 2005. In 2013 it became dual-provider, letting dealers choose between AutoCheck and CARFAX on Autotrader. In June 2025 it made AutoCheck the co-branded provider on all eligible KBB.com and Autotrader.com listings, and secondary analyses describe this as AutoCheck becoming exclusive. The KBB–Experian relationship goes back to at least 2013, and Manheim has worked with Experian since at least 2010.

### Cited Findings
- **2005:** Autotrader and CARFAX announced a "strategic partnership" giving Autotrader used-car shoppers access to free CARFAX Vehicle History Reports. — [Autotrader press release, Sep 6 2005](https://press.autotrader.com/news-releases?item=66711)
- **2013:** Experian renewed its agreement with Kelley Blue Book and partnered with AutoTrader.com. Starting September 2013, dealers who subscribed to AutoCheck could show AutoCheck reports on their Autotrader listings. — [Experian press release 2013](https://www.experianplc.com/newsroom/press-releases/2013/experian-automotive-renews-agreement-with-kelley-blue-book-partners); [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/experian-automotive-renews-agreement-with-kelley-blue-book-partners-with-autotradercom-to-make-autocheck-reports-available-via-top-online-auto-sites-210984411.html); [DealerRefresh, "AutoCheck … on AutoTrader Starting September 2013"](https://www.dealerrefresh.com/autocheck-vehicle-history-reports-on-autotrader-com/); [Auto Remarketing](https://www.autoremarketing.com/ar/technology/autotrader-experian-partnership-allows-dealers-include-autocheck-reports-listings)
- A dealer forum thread from the same period says AutoTrader signed a new agreement with CARFAX at the same time, so dealers could choose either provider. The thread says this ended CARFAX's exclusive arrangement. It also mentions a "$14M" figure, which I could not verify. — [DealerRefresh forum](https://forum.dealerrefresh.com/threads/autotrader-customers-can-now-use-carfax-or-autocheck-now.3552/) (anecdotal)
- AutoCheck reports were also added to KBB's dealer-facing KARPOWER Online platform. — [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/experian-automotives-autocheck-vehicle-history-reports-now-available-on-kelley-blue-books-karpower-online-platform-223875621.html) (date not visible in snippet; probably around 2013)
- **June 10, 2025:** KBB and Autotrader announced an expanded "strategic relationship" with Experian to co-brand AutoCheck vehicle history reports on eligible KBB.com and Autotrader.com listings. Each report includes the AutoCheck Score, which gives a predicted-reliability snapshot. — [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/kelley-blue-book-experian-team-up-to-bring-buyers-better-vehicle-history-reports-302477997.html); [MarketScreener](https://www.marketscreener.com/quote/stock/EXPERIAN-PLC-9590128/news/Kelley-Blue-Book-and-Autotrader-Cox-Automotive-Brands-Announce-Strategic-Relationship-with-Experia-50211377/); [CollisionWeek, Jun 11 2025](https://collisionweek.com/2025/06/11/kelley-blue-book-partners-experian-enhanced-vehicle-history-reports/)
- Experian/Cox claimed that free AutoCheck reports drive "44% more lead conversions on Autotrader vehicle detail pages than the leading competitor." The competitor is not named in the snippets, and the stated analysis window ("Dec 2025 to May 2025") looks like a typo. — [AutoSuccess](https://www.autosuccessonline.com/autocheck-kelley-blue-book-reports-launch/)
- Motor1 described the 2025 deal as "prominently" featuring AutoCheck across all eligible KBB/Autotrader listings and framed it as a challenge to CARFAX's consumer dominance. — [Motor1](https://www.motor1.com/news/762576/kelley-blue-book-autotrader-autocheck-partnership/) (snippet only)
- A Substack analysis says Cox "switched to Experian AutoCheck in June 2025 and made it the exclusive choice on Autotrader.com and KBB.com." — [Platform Professional Substack](https://platformprofessional.substack.com/p/carfaxs-cautionary-tale-for-dpps) (secondary/opinion source)
- **Manheim:** a 2010 Experian release describes expanded vehicle-data services with Manheim. — [Experian 2010](https://www.experianplc.com/newsroom/press-releases/2010/experian-automotive-expands-vehicle-data-service-offerings-with-manheim)

### Inferences
- Cox's shift lines up with CARFAX building its own consumer marketplace (see §4). Commentators frame Cox's move as a reaction to CARFAX becoming a channel competitor and to cost. Neither Cox nor CARFAX was quoted giving a reason in the snippets I saw.
- I did not find a primary source that uses the word "exclusive" for the 2025 Cox–AutoCheck arrangement. The press release says "co-brand … on eligible listings."

### Gaps
- No evidence found of a Cox-built proprietary history or condition report. Manheim condition reports exist but were not researched.
- Contract values, and how much Cox invested in the switch: not found.
- Whether dealers can still attach CARFAX reports on Autotrader after June 2025: not confirmed.
- vAuto and Dealertrack integrations with either provider: not found.

## 2. Other marketplaces and retailers (Cars.com, CarGurus, TrueCar, Edmunds, eBay Motors, Carvana, Vroom, CarMax)

### Takeaway
CARFAX's early-2010s listing-site exclusivity eroded quickly. Experian won dealer-choice or exclusive deals with eBay Motors (2009, exclusive), Edmunds (2012), Autotrader (2013) and Cars.com (2014), and earlier with CarsDirect (2003). By January 2026, AIM Group reported that "almost all major U.S. auto marketplaces" had ended CARFAX VHR tie-ins in favour of the cheaper AutoCheck. Only Cox's switch is well documented in primary sources. The Cars.com, CarGurus and TrueCar changes are not.

### Cited Findings
- **AIM Group, Jan 28 2026:** "U.S. auto marketplaces end tie-ins with Carfax due to competitive element, higher costs." The report says almost all major US marketplaces no longer work with CARFAX for VHRs and that sites and dealers are choosing the cheaper AutoCheck. The article is paywalled, so I saw no site-by-site list. — [AIM Group](https://aimgroup.com/2026/01/28/multiple-marketplaces-end-carfax-tie-ins-with-experian-autocheck-as-replacement/)
- **Cars.com:**
  - CARFAX reports have appeared on subscribing dealers' Cars.com listings since 2005, and the partnership was renewed in 2014. — [Digital Dealer](https://digitaldealer.com/news/carfax-and-cars-com-renew-partnership-to-make-vehicle-history-reports-accessible/58151/); [Vehicle Remarketing](https://www.vehicleremarket.com/116731/carfax-cars-com-renew-partnership)
  - Experian announced on Jan 21 2014 that AutoCheck reports would be accessible on Cars.com used listings, giving dealers a choice of provider. — [Experian 2014](https://www.experianplc.com/newsroom/press-releases/2014/experian-automotive-partners-with-carscom-to-make-autocheck-vehicle-history-reports-accessible); [Digital Dealer](https://digitaldealer.com/news/experian-automotive-partners-with-cars-com-to-make-autocheck-vehicle-history-reports-accessible-on-its-used-vehicle-listings/57987/)
  - Cars.com's own (undated) consumer article now says many listings carry a "Free AutoCheck Report" link. — [Cars.com](https://www.cars.com/articles/how-do-i-get-a-free-carfax-report-420848/)
  - The Substack piece says Cars.com "announced that they would stop using Carfax." It gives no date and no primary citation. — [Substack](https://platformprofessional.substack.com/p/carfaxs-cautionary-tale-for-dpps)
  - A consumer forum thread titled "Carfax gone from cars.com?" exists, but I could not see its date. — [BITOG forum](https://bobistheoilguy.com/forums/threads/carfax-gone-from-cars-com.403512/)
- **eBay Motors (2009):** launched free vehicle history reports on all used listings (model year 1981 onward) through an exclusive agreement with Experian AutoCheck. Sellers could not opt out, per Automotive News as cited. — [Vehicle Remarketing](https://www.vehicleremarket.com/news/ebay-motors-launches-free-vehicle-history-reports-exclusively-through-experian39s-autocheck); [Jalopnik](https://jalopnik.com/ebay-to-offer-free-vehicle-history-reports-starting-wed-5402078); [eBay seller page](https://pages.ebay.com/sellerinformation/news/vehiclehistoryreports.html)
- **Edmunds:** search summaries say Experian made AutoCheck available through Edmunds in 2012 (snippet-level; primary release not seen). Edmunds' own comparison article found CARFAX more detailed but AutoCheck better value, at roughly half the price for multiple reports. — [Edmunds](https://www.edmunds.com/car-buying/which-vehicle-history-report-is-right-for-you.html)
- **CarsDirect (2003)**, **Consumer Guide** and **AutoExtra.com** each selected AutoCheck; Consumer Guide and AutoExtra did so as the "exclusive source" of VHRs. — [F&I Magazine (CarsDirect)](https://www.fi-magazine.com/news/experian-automotive-announces-partnership-with-carsdirect-com); [Auto Dealer Today (Consumer Guide)](https://www.autodealertodaymagazine.com/315272/consumer-guide-selects-experians-autocheck-as-exclusive-source-of-vehicle-histor); [F&I Magazine (AutoExtra)](https://www.fi-magazine.com/news/autoextracom-selects-autocheck-as-exclusive-source-of-vehicle-history-reports)
- **Smaller CARFAX wins (2011–2014):** KSL.com, Vehix, eCarList and Cargigi integrated CARFAX reports. — [Digital Dealer (KSL)](https://digitaldealer.com/news/ksl-com-links-carfax-vehicle-history-reports-to-dealer-listings/45734/); [F&I Magazine (Vehix)](https://www.fi-magazine.com/news/vehix-and-carfax-announce-further-integration); [Auto Remarketing (Cargigi)](https://www.autoremarketing.com/?p=11886)
- **CarMax:** provides a free AutoCheck report on every vehicle in inventory. — [CarMax](https://www.carmax.com/articles/free-vehicle-history-report-walk-through)
- **Carvana:** a third-party guide says most listings include a free CARFAX report. Carvana's own certified-program page, which is several years old, advertises a free CARFAX report and also cites AutoCheck as a data source. — [Carvana](https://www.carvana.com/certified-program); [RideCheckPro](https://www.ridecheckpro.com/carvana-carfax/)
- **CarGurus:** publishes a CARFAX-vs-AutoCheck explainer, but I found no source on which provider its listings currently use. The Substack piece says CarGurus and TrueCar "earlier integrated Carfax." — [CarGurus](https://www.cargurus.com/research/articles/vehicle-history-reports); [Substack](https://platformprofessional.substack.com/p/carfaxs-cautionary-tale-for-dpps)

### Inferences
- Experian's strategy was to win placement wherever CARFAX was not locked in: eBay, CarsDirect and CarMax as exclusives, and Autotrader, Cars.com and Edmunds as dealer-choice options. It then converted the largest marketplace group (Cox) to primary co-branding in 2025.
- The reasons cited are price (AutoCheck is cheaper for dealers and platforms) and CARFAX becoming a competing listings marketplace. These come from AIM Group and Substack commentary, not from statements by the platforms.

## 3. J.D. Power/NADA, Black Book, R.L. Polk, and OEM CPO programs

### Takeaway
I found no evidence that NADA Guides/J.D. Power or Black Book launched their own consumer vehicle history reports. Their role was valuation, and they were integrated alongside CARFAX and AutoCheck. Polk was CARFAX's owner rather than a challenger, and it was in turn bought by IHS in 2013. For CPO programs, the 2013 complaint alleged CARFAX exclusivity with roughly 32–37 brands. The court later found the CPO deals short-term or terminable.

### Cited Findings
- **Black Book and NADA** valuations were integrated into Manheim POINT in 2006. **Black Book** joined POADA alongside Manheim, CARFAX and KBB. — [Auto Remarketing](https://autoremarketing.com/subprime/manheim-point-adds-black-book-s-vehicle-values); [Auto Remarketing (POADA)](https://autoremarketing.com/ar/black-book-laser-appraiser-net-profit-join-poada)
- Autotrader partnered with NADAguides for pricing on listings. Source is a dealer forum thread; date not seen. — [DealerRefresh forum](https://forum.dealerrefresh.com/threads/autotrader-announces-a-partnership-nadaguides-com.11768/)
- **Polk/IHS:** IHS Markit filings state the CARFAX complaint was served on April 23, 2013, before IHS's acquisition of R.L. Polk & Co., CARFAX's parent. — [IHS Markit 10-K FY2016](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001598014/000159801417000024/q41610k.htm)
- **CPO exclusivity alleged:** the 2013 complaint said CARFAX gave marketing support to the makers of 37 car brands in exchange for exclusive use of CARFAX reports in their CPO programs. Another outlet reported 32 of 38 CPO programs. — [Vehicle Service Pros](https://www.vehicleservicepros.com/home/news/10928296/carfax-hit-with-50-million-antitrust-lawsuit-by-120-dealerships); [Constantine Cannon](https://constantinecannon.com/antitrust-group/antitrust-today-blog/auto-dealers-allege-carfax-is-a-competitive-lemon-in-antitrust-suit/)
- The 2014 S.D.N.Y. opinion summarised the plaintiffs' claim that CPO vehicles account for 36% of VHRs sold annually. — [vLex summary of 2014 opinion](https://case-law.vlex.com/vid/mazda-v-carfax-inc-894269742); [appliedantitrust.com PDF of Doc 59](https://appliedantitrust.com/20_exclusive_contracts/exclusive_cases/maxon/maxon_sdny_dismiss_order9_29_2014.pdf)
- **Ford/Lincoln (Oct 2014):** all Ford and Lincoln CPO vehicles were to be listed on CARFAX Used Car Listings, and Ford would continue to provide free CARFAX reports on all its CPO vehicles. — trade press via search summary ([F&I Magazine listing page](https://www.fi-magazine.com/categories/fi?page=311)); primary release not seen.

### Inferences
- The CPO channel was CARFAX's strongest lock-in at the time of the suit. The court's view that these deals were short-term or terminable meant they did not count as durable foreclosure.

- Any J.D. Power or NADA vehicle history product: not found.

## 4. CARFAX's own competitive moves (exclusives, brand advertising, CARFAX Listings, Car Care)

### Takeaway
CARFAX defended its position on several fronts. It signed exclusive deals with the two largest listing sites and most OEM CPO programs. It ran heavy consumer advertising ("Show Me the CARFAX," launched 2009 and described as its largest-ever marketing initiative). It launched its own Used Car Listings marketplace around 2014. It built a free Car Care consumer app (2012) that also gathers service-shop data. Commentators argue the listings marketplace backfired by turning partners into rivals.

### Cited Findings
- **Exclusives:** the complaint alleged that CARFAX's agreements with Autotrader.com and Cars.com meant those sites would post VHRs only from CARFAX, and that listings without a CARFAX link were stigmatised. — [Vehicle Service Pros](https://www.vehicleservicepros.com/home/news/10928296/carfax-hit-with-50-million-antitrust-lawsuit-by-120-dealerships)
- A dealer forum post says Autotrader and Cars.com could not display AutoCheck under their CARFAX arrangements. The same dealer quotes an unlimited CARFAX subscription at about $2,575 (period unclear). — [DealerRefresh forum](https://forum.dealerrefresh.com/threads/opinion-carfax-is-extorting-my-dealership.2902/) (anecdotal)
- **"Show Me the CARFAX":** launched in 2009 as CARFAX's "largest-ever marketing initiative," with nationwide network and cable TV. A 2012 wave was made with Zimmerman Advertising and Hinge Digital. The campaign won a Silver Effie, and CARFAX claimed record unaided awareness. **No ad-spend dollar figure was found**; Winmo's estimate is paywalled. — [Vehicle Remarketing](https://www.vehicleremarket.com/news/carfax-launches-campaign-to-boost-used-car-sales); [Auto Remarketing (Car Fox)](https://www.autoremarketing.com/ar/carfax-launches-new-ad-campaign-featuring-car-fox-character); [Vehicle Remarketing (Effie)](https://www.vehicleremarket.com/79300/show-me-the-carfax-campain-receives-award); [Winmo](https://open.winmo.com/company/carfax)
- **CARFAX Used Car Listings:** soft-launched around early 2014, positioned against Autotrader and Cars.com. One dealer forum user relays a CARFAX rep's claim that the launch was "a result of their fallout from AutoTrader.com" (unverified, secondhand). — [DealerRefresh article](https://www.dealerrefresh.com/carfax-used-car-search-listing-dealers/); [DealerRefresh forum](https://forum.dealerrefresh.com/threads/carfax-now-competing-with-cars-com-autotrader-com.3904/)
- A search summary dated the launch to March 2014 with more than 3 million listings (snippet-level). Current CARFAX messaging says every listing comes with a free CARFAX report. — [About CARFAX](https://www.carfax.com/company/about)
- **Backlash:** partners "resented seeing their high-traffic vehicle detail pages subsidize a rival" marketplace, and some major marketplaces cut ties. — [Platform Professional Substack](https://platformprofessional.substack.com/p/carfaxs-cautionary-tale-for-dpps) (opinion)
- **Car Care:** the app launched in 2012. CARFAX says it has more than 53 million US users (May 2026 release, "50 Million Car Care Users"). CARFAX tells dealers and shops to "join CARFAX Car Care for free to get your service work included on CARFAX Reports." CARFAX cites a network of more than 90,000 dealer and service-shop partners. — [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/carfax-celebrates-50-million-car-care-users-302524036.html); [CARFAX for Dealers](https://www.carfaxfordealers.com/); [About CARFAX](https://www.carfax.com/company/about)
- **Newer badge:** a "Homegrown" badge marks vehicles serviced consistently by one dealership. — [CBT News](https://www.cbtnews.com/carfax-launches-homegrown-badge/)

### Inferences
- The 2013–14 sequence looks causal: Autotrader opened to AutoCheck in Sept 2013, then CARFAX launched its own listings in 2014. That reading rests on forum hearsay and commentary only.
- Car Care is a data-acquisition moat (owner-reported and shop-reported service records) that competing aggregators would have to replicate.

### Gaps
- CARFAX Listings traffic or share versus Autotrader, Cars.com and CarGurus: not found.
- CARFAX ad-spend estimates: not found.
- Competing service-record aggregators were not researched in this pass.

## 5. The dealer antitrust case (Maxon Hyundai Mazda et al. v. CARFAX, S.D.N.Y. No. 1:13-cv-02680-AJN; 2d Cir. No. 16-3717-cv)

### Takeaway
The suit was filed in April 2013 by about 120–121 dealers seeking $50M+ and grew to about 469 plaintiff dealers. It alleged that CARFAX violated Sherman Act §§1–2 through exclusive deals with Autotrader, Cars.com and OEM CPO programs that foreclosed AutoCheck. Judge Alison J. Nathan let most of the case survive a motion to dismiss on Sept 29, 2014. On Sept 30, 2016 she granted CARFAX summary judgment on the dispositive foreclosure question. The Second Circuit affirmed by summary order on June 1, 2018 (726 F. App'x 66). A second dealer suit filed in January 2017 was stayed pending the appeal. No FTC or DOJ action was found.

### Cited Findings
- **Filing:** April 2013 in S.D.N.Y. by Bellavia, Blatt, Andron & Crossett for 121 dealers (some outlets say 120), seeking injunctive relief and $50M in collective damages, trebled under the antitrust laws. The complaint alleged CARFAX held about 90% share. The original caption was "Hyundai Mazda NYLSI Inc. d/b/a Sunrise Toyota et al. v. Carfax." — [F&I Magazine](https://www.fi-magazine.com/news/121-dealers-file-50m-plus-suit-against-carfax); [Vehicle Service Pros](https://www.vehicleservicepros.com/home/news/10928296/carfax-hit-with-50-million-antitrust-lawsuit-by-120-dealerships); [Chron](https://www.chron.com/cars/article/Carfax-gets-sued-for-50-million-on-antitrust-4463080.php); [Jalopnik](https://www.jalopnik.com/carfax-sued-for-50-million-by-120-dealers-in-massive-a-479988420/)
- **Service and plaintiff count:** the complaint was served April 23, 2013. A second amended complaint in October 2013 named about 469 dealers. — [IHS Markit 10-K FY2016](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001598014/000159801417000024/q41610k.htm); [Compass Lexecon](https://www.compasslexecon.com/cases/carfax-litigation/)
- **Market allegations, as summarised in the Sept 29, 2014 MTD opinion:**
  - AutoCheck held about 10% share; eight other providers had negligible shares.
  - AutoCheck's unlimited subscriptions were priced below CARFAX's, but relatively few dealers bought them because of the exclusives.
  - The website agreements foreclosed VHRs for 75% of used cars sold by franchised dealers.
  - The Autotrader deal foreclosed 53% of used vehicles sold by independent dealers.
  - CPO vehicles account for 36% of VHRs sold annually.
  - **These are allegations, not findings.** — [vLex](https://case-law.vlex.com/vid/mazda-v-carfax-inc-894269742); [appliedantitrust.com Doc 59](https://appliedantitrust.com/20_exclusive_contracts/exclusive_cases/maxon/maxon_sdny_dismiss_order9_29_2014.pdf); [Auto Dealer Today, "Judge Allows Antitrust Lawsuit Against CARFAX to Proceed"](https://www.autodealertodaymagazine.com/news/judge-allows-antitrust-lawsuit-against-carfax-to-proceed)
- **Procedure:** the parties agreed to an expedited track. CARFAX moved for partial summary judgment solely on whether its exclusive agreements foreclosed competition in VHR sales, and both sides agreed a CARFAX win would end the whole case. — [Compass Lexecon](https://www.compasslexecon.com/cases/carfax-litigation/); [FindLaw 2d Cir. opinion](https://caselaw.findlaw.com/court/us-2nd-circuit/1936668.html)
- **Summary judgment:** granted by Judge Alison J. Nathan on September 30, 2016 per the IHS 10-K ("September 2016" per Compass Lexecon). F&I Magazine reported the order was released on Dec 9, which suggests it was initially sealed (unconfirmed). Reasoning, per CARFAX's economic consultant (Robert Willig, Compass Lexecon):
  - (a) The CPO agreements were short-term or terminable, so they could not foreclose competition.
  - (b) The website agreements did not foreclose AutoCheck because it could reach consumers through other channels.
  - (c) The plaintiffs' expert's foreclosure estimate was unreliable because it relied on an artificially small figure for CARFAX's sales.
  - (d) Even if the alleged conduct occurred, foreclosure was insufficient to support a claim.
  - Exact foreclosure percentages in the 2016 opinion: not found. — [IHS Markit 10-K FY2016](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001598014/000159801417000024/q41610k.htm); [Compass Lexecon](https://www.compasslexecon.com/cases/carfax-litigation/); [F&I Magazine](https://www.fi-magazine.com/news/mass-action-lawsuit-against-carfax-dismissed)
- **Appeal:** the Second Circuit affirmed on June 1, 2018 (No. 16-3717-cv; 726 F. App'x 66). It held that plaintiffs bear the burden of showing the requisite foreclosure, upheld the district court's critique of the plaintiffs' expert, and noted that summary judgment is "particularly favored" in antitrust cases. Later commentary cites the order for factors such as barriers to entry, and for evidence that some dealers preferred AutoCheck yet stayed CARFAX customers. Arnold & Porter represented CARFAX. — [FindLaw](https://caselaw.findlaw.com/court/us-2nd-circuit/1936668.html); [Law360](https://www.law360.co.uk/articles/1051910/2nd-circ-scraps-auto-dealers-carfax-report-monopoly-suit); [Arnold & Porter](https://www.arnoldporter.com/en/perspectives/news/2018/07/arnold-porter-secures-victory-for-carfax); [Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/pressrelease/34158/arnold--porter-secures-second-circuit-victory-for-carfax); [Network Law Review](https://www.networklawreview.org/wright-krzepicki-foreclosure/)
- **Second suit:** a second dealer group filed a similar suit in January 2017. It was stayed pending the appeal, and IHS Markit expected a similar favourable result. — [IHS Markit 10-Q 2018](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001598014/000159801418000103/q21810q.htm); [IHS Markit 10-Q Q1 2017](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001598014/000159801417000068/q11710q.htm)
- **Related insurance case:** AIG did not have to defend CARFAX in the $50M antitrust suit (coverage litigation). — [Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/unitedstates/antitrust-eu-competition-/686008/aig-won39t-have-to-defend-carfax-in-50-million-antitrust-suit); [Carlton Fields](https://www.carltonfields.com/insights/blogs/propertycasualtyfocus/imported/aig-wont-have-to-defend-carfax-in-50-million-antitrust-suit)
- Docket references: [CourtListener docket 1:13-cv-02680](https://www.courtlistener.com/docket/4352112/maxon-hyundai-mazda-v-carfax-inc/); [Justia Doc 108 (2015)](https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/district-courts/new-york/nysdce/1:2013cv02680/411116/108/)

### Inferences
- The case turned on a narrow point: whether CARFAX's contracts shut out enough of the market. The court found they did not, because the CPO deals were short or terminable, AutoCheck had other ways to reach buyers, and the plaintiffs' expert estimate was unreliable. The court treated CARFAX's dominance as brand and consumer preference rather than contractual lock-up.
- The marketplaces' dual-provider opening in 2013–14 (Autotrader in Sept 2013, Cars.com in Jan 2014) came during the suit and likely weakened the foreclosure case. I found no source that makes this link explicitly.
- I saw "2017" and "2016" given as the appeal date in prompts and summaries. Primary-derived sources (FindLaw caption, IHS 10-Q, Law360, Arnold & Porter) put the affirmance at **June 1, 2018**.

### Gaps
- Exact foreclosure percentages and contract-length findings in the 2016 opinion: full text not retrievable.
- Outcome of the 2017 second suit (likely dismissed or abandoned after the affirmance): not confirmed.
- No FTC or DOJ investigation of CARFAX's exclusive dealing found. No other antitrust suits against CARFAX found.
- Why the order was released Dec 9 (sealing?): unconfirmed.
