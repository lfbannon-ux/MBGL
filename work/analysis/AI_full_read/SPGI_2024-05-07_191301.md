## B. Parent AI-age thesis

### B1. SPGI-WIDE, AI-opportunity, DATA, MOAT
- Speaker: Doug Peterson, President and CEO. Q&A: Manav asked for his general view on GenAI and how S&P benefits on cost or top line.
- Quote: "the secret to GenAI is to have data that's clean, it's easy to use, you can tokenize it, you can feed it into models."
- Detail: Argues the Kensho acquisition (six years ago) built data linking and data management products; benefit on both top line and cost, but "not ready to call it yet". Mobility not named or excluded. Frames clean proprietary data as the key input to GenAI (a pro-data-owner thesis).

### B2. SPGI-WIDE, AI-efficiency, AI-product (governance and firewall)
- Speaker: Doug Peterson, President and CEO
- Quote: "It's a co-pilot that we built internally that allows us to have a firewall around all of the data, so we're protecting all of our data and our intellectual property by bringing the models inside of our firewall, so we can bring in models from anybody, OpenAI, from Microsoft, from AWS, from Anthropic, and we can bring them in."
- Detail: S&P Spark Assist internal co-pilot; vision is that GenAI supplements people, not replaces; Chief AI Officer is also Kensho CEO. Later: runs below $1 per user per month; 1,800 power users with a query library; Kensho benchmark ranks models on 600 standardized questions; open-architecture approach controls cost. Nothing in market yet except ChatIQ (conversational search on CapIQ Pro) in pilot; no numbers. Mobility not named.

### B3. SPGI-WIDE, AI-product, MOAT (domain model), ANALYST-challenge
- Speaker: Doug Peterson, President and CEO. Q&A: Manav asked about S&P's own model versus others.
- Quote: "One of the things that large language models do very poorly is financial calculations."
- Detail: S&P's FinLM is a small language model; LLMs extract language and FinLM layers calculations on top. Argues LLMs alone are weak at financial calcs (CAGRs, multiples, corporate actions), implying a role for S&P's structured data and models. Mobility not named.
