## Event summary
- Mobility Q3 2024 revenue +9% (two of three lines double digit); Dealer +10% on CARFAX subscription growth, Manufacturing +2% (recall transaction revenue still lower), Financials and Other +12%; margin 42.2% flat, TTM 38.8% (-20 bps).
- Full-year guidance tightened: Mobility revenue growth 8%-8.5% and adjusted operating margin 38.5%-39%.
- Vitality: CARFAX listings and the CARFAX Banking and Insurance Group are key contributors; GenAI used to put the animated CARFAX Car Fox into vehicle history reports.
- Interim CFO Craig said Mobility core subscription growth was a little over double digits even with the CDK outage; recall remains the headwind, with an easy lap in 2025.
- Org: Mobility continues under Edouard Tavernier in the new leadership structure (Martina Cheung incoming CEO).
