## Event summary

- Mobility Q2 2022: revenue +7% y/y (planning solutions and used car), 78% recurring, adjusted operating profit +9%, margin +90 bps, trailing-twelve-month margin 39.5%.
- Dealer revenue +10% on CARFAX strength and very high retention; manufacturing +3% with flat OEM marketing spend; financials and other +3% with low auto sales volumes.
- Reinstated 2022 guidance for Mobility: high single-digit revenue growth and high-30s margins. Mobility team expects light vehicle sales to rise next year and beyond, back near pre-pandemic production by 2025, a tailwind for CARFAX and automotiveMastermind.
- Q&A: management argued the used-car/tight-supply environment and dealer demand for data keep growth high, with EV transition, production recovery (up to 9% in 2023) and financial-sector products (Auto CreditInsight) as further drivers.
