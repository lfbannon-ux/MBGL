# D11 — Pre-mortem: how can we lose? (Mobility Global, MBGL)

Prepared 2026-10-06 by the Part D synthesis analyst. It follows `analysis/D_SPEC.md`. **This file synthesizes D1–D10, QUALITATIVE, K_session_tables, TARGETS_TIMELINE and canonical/accounting_notes.csv. No new research was done.** Every figure carries the tier and file reference of the analysis it comes from.

**Tiers:** [D] disclosed · [C] computed (method stated) · [E] estimate (assumption stated) · [IR] management verbal · [X] third party · [context] general knowledge.

**Ground rule for this file:** every bear case must extrapolate something **already observed** in the record. A case that relies only on a hypothetical is excluded or labelled as a watch-item.

**Base for magnitudes [E]**
- FY26 guide midpoint: revenue $1,877.5m and Adj. EBITDA $752.5m [D] (D10 §3).
- "Plan" means the medium-term 7.5–10% organic growth (midpoint 8.75%) [IR] from 2028, plus ~50bp a year of margin expansion [IR] (TARGETS_TIMELINE).
- FY28 plan revenue ≈ $1,877.5m × 1.0875² ≈ **$2,220m** [E]. FY28 plan Adj. EBITDA ≈ **$870m** [E] (D3 §5 inference).
- All magnitudes are gaps against that plan, unless the row says otherwise.

## 2. Risks summary

| Risk | Impact | Likelihood | Comment |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Price lever wears out | **H** | **M** | Price is the largest part of growth (≈55% [C]), and new business is already fading. The brand and OEM CPO mandates support price, but dealer profitability drives it. |
| 2. Cross-sell / GTM failure | M | **H** | Already happened once (the bundle). Integration is only halfway. The 1–2pt bucket is not yet evidenced by any revenue line. |
| 3. AI / search traffic disintermediation | **H** | M | Ad intensity is already rising. Management admits the risk but cannot quantify it. The data leg is safe; the traffic leg is not. |
| 4. B2B commoditisation | M | **H** | B2B growth has been 3–6% for two years, below the plan's high single digits. Low-cost rivals are named by management. |
| 5. Cyclical downturn hits transactional revenue plus price relief | M | M | 2020 is the template: shallow and quickly recovered. A 2008-type shock is untested. |
| 6. Margin target slippage / definitional generosity | M | **H** | Three resets in four months, and a predecessor target missed while revenue was on plan. |
| 7. Leverage / capital-return squeeze | M | M | FCF (~$365m standalone [C]) covers the dividend about 5x. The squeeze is on buybacks and deleveraging speed, not solvency. |
| 8. Data-supply squeeze | **H** | L | The impact would be severe if a major feed were cut. The 10/A already reports fee inflation. No dispute is disclosed. |
| 9. Disclosure retreat | M | **H** (already happening) | It amplifies the other risks and can re-rate the multiple without any change in fundamentals. |
| 10. Incentive misalignment | M | M | It is mostly about what is absent; the first proxy settles it. |
| 11. International / Listings drag | L | M | Small in dollars. The risk is opportunity cost and opaque losses. |

**Inference: correlation.** Cases 1, 2, 4 and 6 are one cluster: "organic growth stays at ~6–7% and margins do not expand". The record already shows the first year of that cluster: the FY26 cut, B2B at 6%, and expansion deferred to 2028. **The most likely way to lose is not a moat collapse. It is a slow re-rating as a 6–7% grower with ~39% margins and 2.7x leverage being priced as a 9–10% compounder.**

## 3. Honest counters: why the bear cases may not bite

1. **The core moat is real, and it is widening by the measures that exist.**
   - Sources grew ~6.5% a year and records ~9.3% a year over FY16–25 [C] (D1 §2).
   - The CARFAX segment Adj. EBITDA margin rose 45.5% → 46.9% → ~48% [C] (D2 (d)).
   - Growth beat industry units in 10 of 11 years [C] (D1 §4.1).
   - Price-led growth with a ~10–15x claimed dealer ROI [IR] and a report cost that keeps falling relative to vehicle price [IR] can run for many years.
2. **Revenue guidance has been broadly reliable.** Two of three SPGI-era years beat the initial revenue guide. The FY26 cut is ~1pt like-for-like [C] (D10 Honest counters).
3. **Subscription revenue held even in 2020** (recurring organic +3% [D]). Downturns have produced price relief, not cancellations, and recovery came within 12 months (D9 §3).
4. **The B2B weakness may be a one-year investment cycle.** 6M-26 B2B Adj. EBITDA flow-through recovered to ~50%, and Q2 margin was 32.7% → 34.0% [C] (D2 (d)).
5. **Several "red flags" cut against the company's own optics.** The Q2-25 recast lowers growth. The 10-Q volunteered the price split. Carve-out EPS shows a −18% decline (D4 Honest counters).
6. **The dividend is easily covered.** It is ~19% of standalone FCF [C] (D3 §5). The "25%" problem is labelling, not cash.
7. **The cost and margin resets are standard spin mechanics.** Standalone costs (~$26m [C]) are ~1.4% of revenue, and SBC moving to peer levels improves alignment (D5 Honest counters).
8. **The AI risk has a counter-argument in the record.** The data sits "behind a firewall or a paywall" [IR], and AI lowers MBGL's own processing costs (D1 §3; D6 §3.1). An answer engine still needs a licensed, trusted source. That could become a new licensing channel rather than a substitute (**Inference**, untested).
9. **Magnitudes are not additive.** Cases #1, #2 and #4 overlap: each describes the same organic shortfall from a different angle. Summing the EBITDA estimates above would double-count.
