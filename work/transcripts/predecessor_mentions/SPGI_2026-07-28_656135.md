## Event summary

- First call after the Mobility spin: Mobility Global completed spinning off on July 1, 2026; Mobility moves to discontinued operations from Q3, so FY guidance (GAAP and adjusted) now excludes Mobility; a July 6, 2026 release provided recast financials ex-Mobility (four quarters of 2025, FY2025 and Q1 2026).
- S&P now has four divisions: Ratings, Indices, Energy, Market Intelligence (451 Research and maritime and trade moved from MI to Energy).
- Excluding Mobility lowers overall revenue growth slightly (Mobility historically higher growth but modestly lower margins) and lifts margins; Ratings and Indices outperformance offsets, so consolidated organic constant currency growth stays 6%-8%; consolidated margin ex-OSTTRA to expand 75-100 bps.
- S&P received about $2 billion dividend from Mobility Global at completion; buybacks raised to over $7 billion for 2026; first-half FCF of $2.4B included Mobility; H2 ex-Mobility FCF guided to $2.9-3.1B.
- No Mobility operating results or CARFAX/dealer commentary on the call.
