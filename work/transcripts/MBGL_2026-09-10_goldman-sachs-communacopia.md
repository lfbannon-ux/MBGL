## Structured summary (analyst's words; [IR] = management statement at this event)

**Setting.** Fireside chat about 10 weeks after the spin, hosted by George Tong (GS).

**Independence benefits [IR].** Management cites better resource prioritisation now that it is outside S&P (investment had tended to go to S&P's core divisions) and the integration of the five businesses. Examples: joint automotiveMastermind-CARFAX product plans, and CARFAX Germany pulled forward from an early-2027 plan to early July 2026 by reusing the CARFAX US platform. Full effect is still "another year or two" away.

**AI / moat [IR].** AI is described as an accelerant: more extracted from existing data feeds, new insights, and new products (VIN-level reliability prediction). The brand becomes more valuable as people worry more about whether data can be trusted. CARFAX is "application agnostic" and embedded in hundreds of third-party applications.

**Growth algorithm [IR].** Medium-term 7.5-10% = core 5-7% + new products 1-2% + international 1-2% (Canada is the largest established international business; Europe includes Italy, Spain, Poland and Germany). Near-term drivers are improving existing products and new products; international is more mid-to-long term. Homegrown and the AMM SMS offers are included in base subscriptions to support future price capture. Showroom is a paid add-on.

**CARFAX go-to-market [IR].** The bundle sale needed two dealership decision-makers and rep incentives encouraged waiting for both. Since June, products are sold individually again with new incentives; it is "a couple of months in" and management is "fairly confident". Listings: US market >$3B, CARFAX <10% share; the 12-18-month opportunity is better monetising existing traffic and growing traffic without hurting the consumer experience. Service Loyalty/Car Care: 53M users, US ambition now ">100 million" (Investor Day said 150M for US+Canada), 1B globally; retention is high once installed.

**B2B [IR].** FAST (self-serve forecast scenarios), PIQ (sourcing/tariff options for OEMs) and Data Studio (OEM + dealer data clean room for incentive targeting) are all early-stage. Several OEMs are on Data Studio, and management wants many more.

**Margins [IR].** FY25 adj. EBITDA margin was 40.6%. The 150bp standalone drag implies a ~39.1% pro-forma base: ~75bp lands in 2026 and the full 150bp in 2027, partly offset by efficiency. The reset should be complete by end-2027, the full 50bp/yr expansion is not expected until 2028, and the medium-term targets effectively start then. Some costs will be reallocated from CARFAX and B2B into corporate, with the segment baseline set over the next 1-2 quarters. AI savings are mostly being reinvested for now. The CEO says margin expansion could exceed 50bp if growth were not the priority.

**Capital [IR].** Started with ~$200M cash. Gross leverage 2.7x, net 2.4x, target ~2.5x gross, reached by delevering through EBITDA growth (no prepayable debt). First dividend paid that day, set at 25% of normalised GAAP NI. Capex is low. "Consistent and meaningful" buybacks start in early 2027. M&A is limited to tuck-ins that accelerate a growth platform or core asset, with nothing meaningful in the short run.
