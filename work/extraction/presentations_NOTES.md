`page` = slide number = Quartr page number. The Investor Day deck states: "All operational and financial metrics ... reflect the period ending December 31, 2025, unless otherwise noted" (slide 3). On that basis, undated KPIs are tagged FY2025. Slides that describe capability (AI, programmes) are tagged `2026` with a blank period_end.

Units: TAM values are converted to usd_m (e.g. $78B → 78000). Counts are converted to absolute `count`. The printed form ("53M +", "~ 832M") is always kept in line_item. Ranges, multiples (x), "+"-suffixed values and bps are stored as `text`. Period codes: `MT` = medium-term target, `STANDALONE` = stand-up cost estimate, `LAUNCH` = anticipated capital structure at spin.

## Internal consistency checks (deck vs deck, and deck vs filings)
- **Q2-2025 Adj. EBITDA was recast.** Investor Day slides 112/114 show $186M. The Q2-26 deck slide 6/11/13 and the 10-Q show $188M. Component differences: interest 3 vs 4, D&A 78 vs 77, and the adjustment lines differ. 1H-25: 170 + 186 = 356 (ID) vs 357 (Q2 deck). Flag this as a restatement between the two decks.
- ID 111 FY23: EBITDA 546 + SBC 20 + non-recurring 30 = 596, but Adj. EBITDA is printed as 598. The slide 113 reconciliation implies EBITDA = 163 + 17 + 61 + 307 = 548. FY24: 608 + 28 + 23 = 659 vs 658 printed. FY25 foots (649 + 22 + 40 = 711). These are printed as-is and not fixed.
- ID 108 FCF bridge: FY23 foots to CFO 393 and FY25 foots to 485. FY24 sums to 426 vs 427 printed (1 off). "Gain (Loss) on Disposition" has only one printed "0". Deferred income taxes are shown as a deduction, as printed.
- ID 112 quarterly: Q1-25 420 − 259 = 161 vs EBITDA 162 printed. Q2-25 173 + 4 + 10 = 187 vs 186. Q4-25 132 + 6 + 23 = 161 vs 162. These are rounding differences. ID 114: the quarterly D&A 78 × 4 = 312 vs FY 310, and interest 3 × 4 = 12 vs 13.
- ID 115: LTM 3/31/26 Adj. EBITDA 724 vs 711 − 170 + 184 = 725 (rounding). Gross leverage of 2.8x at the pro forma $2.0B debt is **above** the <2.5x target on slide 98.
- ID 11 labels "8.6% CAGR" (2023-25). ID 90 labels 8.6% as 2024 organic growth. The two are numerically coincident (1,613 / 1,485 = 8.6%; √(1,750 / 1,485) − 1 = 8.6%).

- TAM basis (27/46/71/47/51/72): "2025 estimates from independent third-party consulting firm; estimated based on midpoint of ranges provided". The firm is not named. The $8T market, 90M+ new vehicles and 1.5B+ VIO figures (12) are "Mobility Global estimates, 2025".
