## Event summary
- Mobility revenue +8% y/y in Q1 2024; Dealer +12% (fifth consecutive double-digit quarter), Manufacturing -3%, Financials and Other +12%.
- Mobility adjusted margin 38.1% (down 100 bps), TTM 38.6% (down 70 bps), with expenses +10% on growth investment and the full-quarter MarketScan effect; adjusted operating profit +5%.
- Full-year Mobility revenue growth guidance reiterated (no numbers given in this call).
- EV transition consulting in Mobility contributed to sustainability and energy transition revenue (+15% to $78M).
- Peterson, on sales cycles: Mobility is undergoing an EV-driven industry transformation and demand for data is steady regardless of sales cycle; EVs piling up in ports and dealer lots create demand for new dealer/OEM products.
