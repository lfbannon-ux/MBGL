**Tiers:** [IR] management; [X] analyst; [C] my computation; **Inference:** my judgement.

## 1. Bottom line: what was said

1. **(1) MBGL names four assets and calls AI an "accelerant".** "It's our data estate, it's our brands, it's the network that we've built, and it's how embedded we are throughout the industry." (Eager, CEO, MBGL Investor Day, 2026-05-12) [Q][id999]
   - Operating AI metrics were given in May, then dropped by September.
   - No AI revenue, no AI savings in dollars, no MCP customers.
2. **(2) The parent described Mobility's advantage consistently, in rising terms.** It ran from "critical must-have products" (2021) to "a very strong competitive moat" (April 2025); speakers and links are in §3.
   - The single concession (Dec 2022): rivals could replicate the data, given time and effort.
   - Retention was never given as a number.
3. **(2) The parent linked Mobility and AI only about eight times, all as efficiency, distribution or brand.** It never called AI a threat to Mobility, and never cited AI as a reason for the spin.
4. **(3) The parent's thesis never named Mobility as a member.** The thesis: models commoditise, proprietary data and benchmarks win, data stays behind firewalls and is not for training.
   - From April 2025 it was stated for the four divisions S&P kept.
   - The spin was justified by customer fit.
5. **Analysts' challenges on AutoCheck, data exclusivity and retention got qualitative answers.** No analyst in 68 events asked whether AI assistants could sit between shoppers and CARFAX.

## 4. What was said about AI

### 4a. Mobility Global management, 2026 (voice 1)

**Thesis [IR]:**
- "AI's not replacing the need for data. It's actually increasing it." (Morehouse, Investor Day) [Q][id6724]
- "In an age of AI, when people don't know what to trust, who to trust, and what they can believe in, we're seeing our brands become a bigger and bigger part of our value proposition." (Eager, Investor Day) [Q][id1194]
- "As AI generates more intelligence from our data, the value of our data only grows." (Eager, Q2-26) [Q][q554]
- "…our differentiation comes from the combo of that brand and data, not the application itself." (Eager, Goldman Sachs, 2026-09-10) [Q][gs798]

**The four engines** ("One, drive productivity. Two, amplify our data." [Q][id1700]):

| Engine | Numbers given at the Investor Day [IR] |
|---|---|
| Productivity | "Today, 84% of those queries are deflected into an AI system…" [Q][id7116]<br>Engineering: +10–15% in 2025, then "four to five times" that in Q1-26 [Q][id7188] |
| Data amplification | 99.5% production accuracy decoding service records; accident records ingested within 2 hours |
| Core products | 25m AI-made Car Fox videos a month [Q][id1879]<br>Behavior Prediction Score: +10% loyalty [Q][id3702]<br>FAST: "automating 90% of the workflow" [Q][id3816]<br>Data Studio: close rates +40% [Q][id3975] |
| New products / agents | "…AI agents in the future, deployed by us or our customers, are going to be operating the workflows we're touching today." (LaFeir) [Q][id3474]<br>Data to be exposed via MCP: "We're designing infrastructure to monetize those agents." [Q][id7570] No customers, pricing or revenue given. |

**Also said at the Investor Day:**
- On cost: "We can't just throw all this at an LLM. I think it's very expensive." [Q][id7783]
- On the team: 100+ dedicated data scientists and AI experts [Q][id6932].
- On margins: a medium-term "50 basis point margin expansion or more" comes from several levers, of which AI is one. The AI share is unclear [Q][id8560].

**What disappeared between May and September 2026 [C]:**
- **Operating metrics:** none recurs on the Q2 call or at Goldman Sachs, and the Head of AI did not speak at either.
- **Data rights:** "The data we have isn't scraped, it's not publicly available, and it's not synthetic." [Q][id6809] became "the majority of it's sitting behind a firewall or a paywall somewhere" [Q][gs251].
- **Switching costs:** "way too high" became brand trust under "data overload" [Q][gs420].
- **Margin from AI:** savings are now reinvested, with "…AI is going to be a big part of that." [Q][gs1809]
- **Still unlaunched:** VIN-level reliability prediction, still described as an opportunity [Q][gs332].

## 7. Assessment (Inference)

1. **Contributor network:** probably strengthened. AI cuts the cost of decoding records more than the cost of collecting them. Its weak points are on the record:
   - Peterson's "publicly available data … from DMVs".
   - Exclusivity is "a mix".
   - The 2021 VIN-ownership question was never closed.
2. **Longitudinal archive:** durable ("doesn't exist anymore anywhere else"). But it is an asset that accumulates, not a barrier to new collection.
3. **Institutional embedding:** robust. This covers OEMs that require a CARFAX report to certify a car, lenders, courts and dealers' pricing engines. AI changes the interface, not the requirement, so "application agnostic" is coherent here.
4. **Consumer brand pull:** least tested. Dealers pay because shoppers ask for CARFAX, and nobody has asked what happens when shoppers ask an assistant instead.
   - Advertising rose 31% over FY23–25 against revenue +18% [C, from D2].
   - The Form 10/A flags rising customer-acquisition costs [D].
5. **B2B applications:** most exposed.
   - Management locates differentiation in "brand and data, not the application".
   - It concedes "a low-cost alternative" [Q][id9337], and its "the only real alternative is a spreadsheet" [Q][id3763] is the gap AI closes.
   - B2B grew only +4% in Q2-26.
6. **AI as a cost lever:** operationally credible, but unpriced.

**Net:** the physical, contractual and institutional layers are strongest. Demand pull is weakest, and it is the layer nobody has asked about.

**Honest counters:**
- Decision-grade data may well gain value: S&P reports retention up 200–500 bp for its AI-channel users [IR], though not for Mobility.
- Many CARFAX reports are required by rule, not chosen.
- The 2022 concession was about time and effort, and AI does not shorten 40 years of physical collection.
- That nobody asked about intermediation may reflect informed holders judging it immaterial.
- The Q2-26 miss was plausibly MBGL's own go-to-market change.

## 8. Questions for management

1. **Traffic.** How do CARFAX visits and reports split between direct, dealer websites, search and AI assistants, and how has that changed since 2024? Has any AI platform asked to license the data?
2. **Data rights.** What share of records comes from exclusive or multi-year sources, and what is annual churn among sources? Do contracts bar AI training, as S&P's do?
3. **Agents.** How many customers use MCP or agents, at what price, and how much of the 1–2% of growth from new products depends on them?
4. **B2B.** Which automotiveMastermind or Polk products face substitutes that OEMs build in-house or that AI-native firms offer? What is B2B gross retention?
