## Event summary
- Asked directly whether Mobility fits S&P's "powering global markets" mission, Peterson defends it as an attractive, top-line-growth, must-have-data business for OEMs, dealers and the supply chain, with synergies across supply chain and sustainability.
- He frames the auto sector as undergoing a 10-year transformation (autonomous, EV, hydrogen, hybrid).
- He does not announce any separation: he says portfolio reviews occur a couple of times a year with the board, and names only Fincentric as a business now being looked at for divestiture.
- Mobility appears in the Vitality Index discussion: new indices and dealer-market products are growing very fast.
- No Mobility financial figures (growth, margin) were given in this event.

### 2
- Speaker / section: Doug Peterson, President and CEO; Q&A. Moderator notes Mobility is a fabulous business with strong top-line growth but not obviously aligned with the mission of powering global markets, and asks how he thinks about it from a portfolio optimization perspective.
