## Event summary
- Mobility Q3 2023: revenue +10% y/y (organic constant-currency +8.2%); third straight quarter of double-digit Dealer growth. Dealer +13% on price realization, new store growth, Carfax for Life, used-car subscription products and the MarketScan acquisition. Manufacturing +4% on elevated recall activity and marketing solutions; Financials and other +9%.
- Margin was slightly pressured: adjusted expenses +10% (incentive comp and MarketScan), adjusted operating profit +10%, margin -20 bps y/y, TTM margin -100 bps.
- Outlook: continued strong growth in used-car subscription products in Q4, plus Mobility benefiting from dealers and OEMs raising incentive spend on new vehicles as high rates hurt affordability.
- Product/AI: Mobility vehicle forecast and registration data launched on the Market Intelligence Marketplace (Express Feed and Snowflake), flagged as an early AI integration opportunity; Mobility data also feeds the real asset data set and energy transition products, and an Automotive Value Chain Carbon Accounting solution.
- M&A: MarketScan acquisition contributed $5.6m of revenue adjustment in Mobility's organic calc. No spin or separation talk.
