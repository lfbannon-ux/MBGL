## What is claimed

- AI is an "accelerant", not a threat: it increases the value of proprietary data (Q2: "the value of our data only grows"; GS: "accelerant") and helps extract more from existing feeds, produce new insights and new products.
- Moat pillars as stated: Q2 prepared close lists proprietary data, trusted brands, scaled customer network, embeddedness; GS adds data estate "purposefully built" for consumer questions and presence in "hundreds" of applications.
- Application layer is not the moat: "application agnostic... differentiation comes from the combo of that brand and data, not the application itself" (GS).
- Brand gains value in the AI age because of "data overload" and trust (GS: brand "becoming more and more valuable").
- Data is behind paywalls/firewalls and not readily available (GS); B2B longitudinal data (10-20 years) "doesn't exist anymore anywhere else" (Q2).
- New AI-related products: VIN-level future reliability (GS, opportunity only); automotiveMastermind behavior prediction score and SMS offers, Data Studio, FAST, PIQ (all "early stages").
- AI efficiency: faster product development, data ingestion; next wave of efficiency (Q2 ad-spend answer); savings reinvested in the short to medium term, with AI "a big part" of margin improvement later (GS).
- Competition is generic: "different competitors at each stop"; "pockets of increased competition"; Listings is a four-player field in which CARFAX is the newest; Advantage position is "highly pinned" in franchise dealers.
- The revenue miss is attributed to own go-to-market execution and softer non-US activity, not competition.

## What is quantified vs not

Quantified (all numbers given on AI, moat or competitive position):
- Data/moat: 20 years of data and "connection points" (Q2); seven, 12, 15-year-old data (Q2); data feeds "over the last 20, 30 years" (GS); CARFAX "north of 40 years" in business (GS).
- Position: 40 of the top 40 manufacturers (Q2); "hundreds and hundreds and hundreds" of applications (GS); Listings "four players", CARFAX newest (Q2); less than half of franchise Advantage dealers on Service Loyalty; average dealer 1.5-2 products (Q2, Calderone; also in Tong's GS question); Listings US market north of $3 billion and CARFAX under 10% (GS); 53 million Car Care users, US target north of 100 million, one billion vehicle-owning consumers globally (GS); 3,200 automotiveMastermind dealers (Q2); about 90 million new cars a year (GS); VIN reliability horizon "next three years" (GS).
- Financial context on the moat: Q2 revenue $468M +7%; subscription +7%; CARFAX +8% (subscription ~+8%, transactional ~+9%); B2B +4% (subscription ~+6%, transactional -4%); FY26 revenue guide cut to 6.9%-7.7%; medium-term 7.5%-10% growth (core 5%-7%, new products 1%-2%, international 1%-2%); 50 bp annual margin expansion not reached until 2028.
