## Event summary
- Mobility is mentioned as a source of revenue synergies, a growth pocket and the fastest-growing S&P segment, with only one dedicated Q&A exchange.
- FY2023 Mobility revenue growth is guided at 8.5%-10.5%, with the Market Scan acquisition adding about 2 points; Steenbergen guides 7%-9% growth for 2025-2026.
- CARFAX: management says the market is not saturated and notes many new product launches.
- The other leg of Mobility is OEM-facing: the shift from combustion to hybrid/EV, with supply chain, predictive analytics and planning models (battery sourcing, factory siting).
- No spin or separation commentary, and no margin figures specific to Mobility.

### 3. Ewout Steenbergen, EVP and CFO - Q&A (two turns)
- Question: George Tong notes Mobility is the fastest-growing segment, guided at 8.5%-10.5% for the full year, and asks how sustainable high-single/low-double-digit growth is and what fuels it.
- Quote: "CARFAX, a lot of new product launches. There is sometimes a misperception that the market is saturated for CARFAX."
- Detail: Reminds that Market Scan, acquired at the start of 2023, is a pricing engine for more direct-to-consumer vehicle sales and contributed about 2 percentage points to this year's growth, so the organic number should be adjusted for it. The company is still guiding to 7%-9% growth in 2025 and 2026. He calls the business very entrepreneurial, with a team that knows its customers and markets and keeps proposing innovation. CARFAX has many new product launches, and he rejects the view that the CARFAX market is saturated: many products still have large market opportunities. The other part of Mobility is OEM exposure to the industry shift from combustion engines to hybrid and EV vehicles. S&P helps OEMs with supply chain, predictive analytics and planning models, including where to source batteries and where to place factories, which he expects to benefit from deep customer involvement. Concludes Mobility should see continued healthy growth.
