## Event summary

- Mobility Q4 2024 revenue +9% (CARFAX suite, insurance products); dealer +10%, manufacturing +1% (recall headwind), financials and other +18%; adjusted expenses +7%; segment profit +12%, margin 34.7% (+100 bps) in Q4, 39.0% (+20 bps) for the full year.
- Recall transaction headwind began early 2024 and is expected to be fully lapped by Q2 2025.
- 2025 guidance: Mobility revenue growth 7%-8.5%, margin 39%-40% (about +50 bps at midpoint), with reinvestment pace slowing.
- Q&A: recall impact small and confined to manufacturing; CARFAX opportunities, strong Market Scan integration, good dealer flows; vehicle affordability a minor headwind that also creates opportunities for incentive/marketing and planning solutions.
- Cheung characterized 2024 Mobility as strong, steady growth despite market headwinds.
