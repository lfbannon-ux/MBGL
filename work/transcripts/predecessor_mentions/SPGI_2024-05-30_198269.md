## Event summary
- Press speculation about a possible Mobility sale/spin was put to Peterson directly; he did not address it but made a strong case that Mobility is an "incredibly attractive" business and a "perfect fit" for S&P Global.
- Rationale: a rare publicly listed benchmark in the mobility sector, with supply chain, long- and short-term forecasting, and industry-transformation data that OEMs and suppliers depend on; exposure to EV and autonomous mobility trends.
- Recent investments in technology, marketing and sales, plus the MarketScan acquisition, which added consumer transaction price data to give a complete picture of consumer behavior for dealers and OEMs.
- No financial figures or guidance for Mobility were given. The only other reference is the moderator's introduction naming "automotive" as one of S&P Global's markets.

### 2. Doug Peterson, President and CEO - Q&A
- Analyst question (moderator): given media speculation (which he can't comment on), how does he see the Mobility business - is it attractive, how does it fit, and what are the opportunities and challenges?
- Quote: "Yeah, the mobility business is an incredibly attractive business. There's no other business that's really that combination of capabilities in the market right now, especially one that's owned by a publicly traded company."
- Detail: Peterson said most similar mobility-type businesses are privately owned, so S&P provides the public-market benchmark for the sector. Mobility offers information on the supply chain, the future of the automotive industry, and long- and short-term forecasting. OEMs and suppliers rely on it to decide what to buy and build and to forecast. He noted the industry is undergoing massive transformation. He did not comment on the speculation itself.

### 3. Doug Peterson, President and CEO - Q&A (continued)
- Analyst question: same as entry 2.
- Quote: "It's a perfect fit for S&P Global."
- Detail: Peterson said the shift to electric vehicles and autonomous mobility puts Mobility at the forefront and cutting edge of data, analytics and research for decisions.
