## Structured summary (analyst's words; [IR] = management statement at this event)

**Setting.** First earnings call after the 1 July 2026 spin. Q2 numbers are carve-out results (pre-spin). Guidance now covers only revenue and adjusted EBITDA.

**Results [IR].** Q2 revenue $468M, +7% (all organic), modestly below plan. Subscription +7%, transactional +5%. CARFAX +8% (subscription ~8%, transactional ~9%, down from 10% in Q1). B2B +4%, down from 8% in Q1 (subscription ~6%, transactional -4%; ~$1M of recall revenue slipped to H2; some Planning project delays; tough Sales Solutions comp; FX). H1 revenue +7.4% (~$5M FX benefit; ~6.8% on the other basis). Adj. EBITDA $202M (+7%), margin 43.2% (+40bp). H1 adj. EBITDA $386M (+8%), margin 42% (+20bp). GAAP NI $53M (-18%) including $36M of one-time separation costs.

**What went wrong [IR].** (1) The CARFAX go-to-market shift in late 2025 to selling Advantage, Listings and Service Loyalty as a "Lifetime" bundle made sales cycles longer, because the sales/GM buyer and the service-manager buyer both had to agree. In June, management went back to selling products individually and changed rep incentives. (2) Weaker auto activity outside the US hit transactional revenue in B2B and CARFAX Canada. CARFAX Europe grew strongly at double digits, but transactional revenue missed. Management says the Middle East had an effect only "on the margins".

**Guidance [IR].** FY26 revenue cut to $1.87-1.885B (+6.9-7.7%), from the 7.5-9% reaffirmed at Investor Day. Adj. EBITDA $745-760M (~40% margin at the midpoint). No macro improvement assumed. H2 margins step down on seasonality (pricing early in the year, ad spend and renewals later) and on standalone costs: a run-rate ~150bp hit to margin vs the FY25 base (the high end of $20-25M), about half of it in 2026. One-time spin costs ~$100M (about half in 2026, ~50% capitalised). H2 interest expense ~$55M, with the first $60M cash coupon in Q4. GAAP tax rate 28-30.5%. Diluted shares 295-297M. Medium-term 7.5-10% growth and 50bp margin expansion reaffirmed. Adjusted NI/EPS reporting starts next quarter. A broader FY27 guide is promised.

**Balance sheet & capital [IR].** Cash $186M, net debt $1.8B, net leverage 2.4x. $2B bonds. Cash taxes ~$89M/yr above provision for 12 years (IHS Markit amortization), plus federal taxes previously paid by SPGI. First quarterly dividend $0.06/share, set on normalised net income, keeping Dividend Aristocrat status. Buybacks from early 2027. No material M&A until fully separated.

**Products & strategy [IR].** CARFAX Homegrown (dealer-sold-and-serviced used vehicles, inside Advantage) and CARFAX Showroom (first premium listing). automotiveMastermind SMS service-lane offers. CARFAX Germany launched early July, ahead of plan, plus Italy and Spain. One Mobility is "about halfway", with ~2 years to complete. Joint AMM-CARFAX roadmap: activate offers for AMM's 3,200 dealers through the 53M Car Care users. Fewer than half of franchise Advantage dealers use Service Loyalty. Average dealer has 1.5-2 products. CARFAX is the newest of the four listings players. B2B goal: lift underlying subscription growth from ~6% to high single digits.
