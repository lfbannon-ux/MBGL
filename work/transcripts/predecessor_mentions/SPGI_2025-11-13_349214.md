## Event summary
- The event was presented as "S&P Global as it will exist after the spin of Mobility": the company reaffirmed the April announcement that it intends to spin Mobility into an independent public company and said it is on track to complete the spin on the original timeline.
- All financial targets and the strategy discussion exclude Mobility in all periods and cover four divisions (Market Intelligence, Ratings, Energy, Indices); a company revenue base of about $13B is cited for the four businesses.
- Reporting changes: MI and Energy business-line reporting will be realigned around the time of the spin (next summer), with a full restatement; no detailed forward divisional margin guidance going forward.
- Medium-term targets (ex-Mobility): 7%-9% organic constant-currency revenue growth, 50-75 bps annual margin expansion, double-digit EPS growth; MI 6%-8%, Ratings 6%-9%, Energy 6%-8%, Indices 10%-12%.
- On Mobility specifically, the only Q&A content was a margin-math clarification: exclude Mobility, with some relatively immaterial stranded costs. No CARFAX, Polk, automotiveMastermind or Mobility operating metrics were discussed.
