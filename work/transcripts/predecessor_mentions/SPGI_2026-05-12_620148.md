## Event summary
- Mobility Global (S&P Global's Mobility division: CARFAX plus the B2B Polk/automotiveMastermind/planning businesses) plans to complete its spin from S&P Global to become an independent public company in mid-2026. The company has filed a Form 10, announced a board and leadership team, secured a $500M revolver, expects to issue about $2B of bonds (mostly dividended to S&P) and to launch with $150M+ of cash, targeting investment grade and gross leverage of 2.5x or below.
- Scale and mix: revenue of about $1.75B (2025 growth 8.5%), about 65% CARFAX and 35% B2B (over $600M, 82% subscription; CARFAX over 80% subscription). Over 90% of revenue is driven by proprietary data. 40,000+ CARFAX dealers, 53M consumers in the Car Care app (goal 150M US/Canada, 1B global), 40 of 40 top OEMs, 94 of top 100 suppliers; 3,400 employees.
- Guidance and targets: 2026 organic growth guide reaffirmed at 7.5%-9%; medium-term (2028+) organic growth 7.5%-10% (core 5%-7%, mostly value), adjusted EBITDA growth 8%-11%, margin about 40% historically with a brief reset from $20M-$25M of standalone costs, 3%-4% equity compensation and $75M-$100M one-time costs, then 50 bps+ expansion; return 75%+ of FCF (dividend payout 20%-25% of net income; buybacks not before 2027); medium-term tax rate 25%-27%; NRR meaningfully above 100%.
- Strategy and products: integrating five legacy businesses into one; cross-sell between B2B (automotiveMastermind in about 3,200 dealers vs CARFAX 40,000) and CARFAX; CARFAX Lifetime Program (1,600 dealers, +19 pts service retention); Behavior Prediction Score, FAST (90% workflow automation, 4x forecast quality), Data Studio/EEQ (40% close-rate improvement); AI (99.5% model accuracy, agentic/MCP, Car Fox video product with 25M views per month); international (Germany for CARFAX in 2026, China OEM expansion, India acquisition).
- Risks and caveats: recall revenue is the one weak line (non-recurring); affordability and 84-month loans; standalone cost reset; competition framed as fragmented by lifecycle stage.
