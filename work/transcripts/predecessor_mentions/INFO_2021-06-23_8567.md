## Event summary
- Transportation organic growth was 39% in Q2 2021 (38% recurring, 41% non-recurring), flattered by the Q2 2020 COVID comparison (pricing concessions, depressed auto trading); EBITDA $171M at 49.6% margin, up 870 bps FX-adjusted.
- Dealer businesses (CARFAX and automotiveMastermind) described as growing rapidly amid tight new and used inventory and soaring used-car prices; CARFAX consumer and dealer transactions drove non-recurring strength.
- FY2021 Transportation organic growth guidance raised to 14-16% from 13-15%; management frames the underlying run-rate as high single digits, not reliant on used or new sales alone.
- Margin gains are partly a "false economy" from temporary 2020 cost cuts (including CARFAX marketing pullback) reversing; margins expected to moderate.
- Connected-car data: POCs at CARFAX and for OEM dealer-network work; real leverage 2-3 years away.
- S&P Global merger expected to close calendar Q4 2021; Automotive/Transportation seen as having no major overlap with S&P, led by Tavernier.
