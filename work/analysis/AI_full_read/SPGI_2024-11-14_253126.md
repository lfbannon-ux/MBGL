### B2. SPGI-WIDE, MOAT (differentiation vs LLMs), ANALYST-challenge response
- Speaker: Martina Cheung, CEO
- Quote: "the reason why it's differentiated is that if you are looking for any of the models right now to return responses that require the models to go into tables, let's say, in a financial statement, the accuracy is really challenged."
- Detail: Positions S&P's value as fixing a weakness in general models; Kensho spent about six months on it. Anecdote: a very large client's head of research described the effort of training a model on its research while "having a problem with getting the right responses back for data that was in tables in their research"; the next day S&P released the AI-ready dataset. Implies customers are building in-house models on top of vendor data rather than being replaced by them.
