## Event summary
- Mobility Q4 2025 revenue +8%: Dealer +10% (CARFAX and automotiveMastermind new customers), Manufacturing +1% (tariff/regulatory uncertainty, fewer recalls), Financials and Other +11%; margin +70 bps to 35.4%.
- Spin: Mobility Global is the chosen name (announced at NADA); Form 10 confidentially filed; senior leadership named (Matt Hoisch CFO designate). Q2 2026 milestones: public Form 10, Investor Day, equity roadshow and an investment-grade public debt offering. Timing of the spin remains uncertain.
- Guidance: 2026 guidance consolidates Mobility; on completion S&P will recast 2025 quarters and 2026 periods ex-Mobility and issue updated guidance plus GAAP guidance. 2026 Mobility organic constant-currency growth guided to 7.5%-9%, with only modest Manufacturing growth assumed.
