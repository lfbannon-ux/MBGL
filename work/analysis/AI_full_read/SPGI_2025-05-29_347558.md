## A. Mobility-specific

### A1. MOAT (vertical SaaS), DATA, COMPETITION, switching
- Speaker: Martina Cheung, President and CEO. Q&A: moderator asked why spin Mobility now and the strategic rationale.
- Quote: "The Mobility business we view as a deep vertical SaaS-based business serves the entire automotive value chain all the way from suppliers to OEM to dealerships and to the end consumer."
- Detail: Mobility grew 8.4% last year at a 39% margin; consistently innovates at high levels. About 70% of the business relates to the used car market; the largest used-car revenue customer type, and the largest number of customers, are dealers.

### A2. COMPETITION/portfolio fit (why not a moat for the parent)
- Speaker: Martina Cheung, President and CEO

## B. Parent AI-age thesis

### B1. SPGI-WIDE, AI-threat (disintermediation), MOAT, ANALYST-challenge
- Speaker: Martina Cheung, President and CEO. Q&A: moderator posed the bear case that agentic AI could autonomously gather, interpret and synthesize financial/market data and disintermediate S&P's Market Intelligence platform.
- Quote: "while it may be possible to generate a data set on financials, and by the way, Yahoo has been doing that for, I don't know, for how long, it's maybe not as possible to integrate that data set using metadata and identifiers and connectors to other more critical data sets that may not be as easy to get."
- Detail: Value is in linking and breadth: "It may also not be easy to get the 10 data sets you need to make a decision". Directed at Market Intelligence; Mobility neither included nor excluded.
