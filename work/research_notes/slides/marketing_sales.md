6. **Recall**: safety-recall owner identification and outreach for US OEMs and dealers.

## 2. Lay explanation (for a 15-year-old)

Marketing & Sales helps car companies and car dealers work out **who is likely to buy a car next, what they will buy, and what deal will win them**. Then it helps them reach those people and checks whether the advertising worked. It knows who owns which car, because every car is registered with the state, and it knows what deals and prices are on offer.

- **Example 1 (automotiveMastermind):** A Ford dealer's software flags that "Sam has a 3-year-old Expedition, owes $32,000 on it, and it's worth $45,000, so offer him the new model." The dealer texts Sam a personalised upgrade offer. (This scenario is the one ex-Cox expert #2 described, p.6.)
- **Example 2 (Polk market reporting):** Toyota wants to know what share of SUVs it sold in Collin County, Texas last quarter, and how many Toyota owners switched to Honda. Polk counts it from registration records.
- **Example 3 (Market Scan / Recall):** When you build a car payment on a dealer's website, Market Scan's engine calculates the correct monthly payment, including rebates and taxes. When an airbag recall is issued, the Recall service finds the cars' current owners so the carmaker can send them letters.

## 3. How it makes money

- **Mostly subscriptions:** "B2B Marketing & Sales solutions (automotiveMastermind, Market Reporting, Market Scan API priced by dealer rooftops)" are sold on "monthly, annual or multi-year subscriptions" [D, 10-Q p.20].
- **Transactional part:** "Marketing & Sales recall campaign outreach that is volume-based". Transactional elements also include "marketing campaigns, VIN pulls, and Recall outreach" [D, 10-Q pp.19–20]. The B2B segment as a whole is 18% transactional [D-deck s.34]; the line-level mix is not disclosed.
- **Go-to-market:** top-down "enterprise agreements with OEMs and dealer groups", led by market reporting and then upselling analytics, audiences and pricing; bottom-up direct sales to individual dealers (aM, Market Reporting, Market Scan APIs, Polk audiences) [D, 10/A p.381].
- **Pricing evidence:** Market Scan is priced per dealer rooftop [D, 10-Q p.20]. aM "matured into a scaled dealer sales platform with steady price discipline" [D, 10/A p.378]. Data Studio deal sizes are shown only as illustrative tiles ($43.5k, $46k, $48k) on deck s.50; the deck does not say they are real contract values.
- **Recall economics:** "In terms of the recall activity, it's actually a very high margin business that comes through." Chris Craig, Interim CFO, S&P Global, Q2-24 call, 30 Jul 2024 [IR] ([link](https://web.quartr.com/companies/3977?companyId=3977&documentId=2727355&documentType=transcript&eventId=188627&navigation=external&utm_medium=referral&utm_source=mcp&targetTime=3434.45))

## 6. Moat in plain words

1. **The registration pipe is hard to copy.** State DMV data needs vetting, contracts, archives and decades of state-by-state cleaning. Polk "got in with the DMV integrations early on".
   - > "A lot of organizations have tried to come in over the years and acquire that data. It's very complicated. There's trust issues at play. There's legislation issues at play." LaFeir, ID26 [IR] ([10052.92s](https://web.quartr.com/companies/22709?companyId=22709&documentId=3659313&documentType=transcript&eventId=676565&navigation=external&utm_medium=referral&utm_source=mcp&targetTime=10052.92))
   - > "Registration data itself, I know of no other company than Polk." [X] Expert call #4, Nissan NMAC finance manager / ex-CARFAX rep, 23 Jun 2026, p.10
   - > "I don't think so. Polk has just historically been the one that got in with the DMV integrations early on, and probably has become the primary pipeline out." [X] Expert call #2, ex-Cox Automotive AVP Product (now iPacket), 9 Jul 2026, p.7. He was asked whether anyone besides Polk offers this data.
2. **History nobody else has.** > "When you look at a lot of that data, some of that data that's seven years old, 12 years old, 15 years old just doesn't exist anymore anywhere else." Bill Eager, CEO, Q2-26 call, 7 Aug 2026 [IR] ([3497.87s](https://web.quartr.com/companies/22709?companyId=22709&documentId=4111696&documentType=transcript&eventId=704668&navigation=external&utm_medium=referral&utm_source=mcp&targetTime=3497.87))
3. **Industry referee.** Polk numbers are what OEMs quote in their adverts and what courts accept: "When industry disputes come up, it's our data that's been tested in court and considered the truth." LaFeir, ID26 ([3152.31s](https://web.quartr.com/companies/22709?companyId=22709&documentId=3659313&documentType=transcript&eventId=676565&navigation=external&utm_medium=referral&utm_source=mcp&targetTime=3152.31)). The 10/A calls the datasets the "'gold standard'" (p.381).
4. **Embedded in workflow.** Market Scan sits inside ~10,500 dealers' software [D]. aM is "the benchmark in the industry" [X #2, p.5].

**Weak spots in the moat:** Polk data lags ("it takes 90 days", [X #2, p.7]). aM adoption may stall near half the franchise base: "Every provider seems to run to about 7,000 or 8,000 dealers in the auto industry and then start to stall out, because that's half the franchise base." [X #2, p.6]. The repo's D14 notes that FY25 B2B revenue rose while Adj. EBITDA fell ($203m→$197m [D]), so the numbers show no pricing power.
